## 1. The Archetype Adaptation

The six foundational engine classes are adapted into visceral, setting-grounded insurgent roles:

```text
ENGINE CLASS      LORE ARCHETYPE                  ROLE & SPECIALIZATION
[ Warrior ]  ===> The Vanguard (Garrison Deserter)       [ Tank / Posture Breaker / Chokepoint Control ]
[ Rogue ]    ===> The Gutter-Stalker (Curfew Runner)     [ Scout / Assassin / Umbral Phase ]
[ Ranger ]   ===> The Arbalestier (Tollhouse Poacher)     [ Ranged Interrupter / Pin / Volley Denial ]
[ Brawler ]  ===> The Slag-Brute (Bone-Mill Laborer)      [ Brawler / Stagger Engine / Line Breaker ]
[ Priest ]   ===> The Heretic Acolyte (Defrocked Scribe)  [ Field Surgery / Strain Siphon / Flesh Weaver ]
[ Mage ]     ===> The Ichor Alchemist (Hedge Occultist)   [ Volatile Chem / Area Blight / Mutagen Flasks ]
```

Every class splits into two distinct, opposing talent paths:
- **Branch A: Cold Steel (The Iron Discipline):** Pure human leverage, heavy weapons, stance mastery, 0% Strain generation, zero permanent calcification.
- **Branch B: Inoculated (The Marrow Graft):** Harvested aberration organs, low AP cost, high burst/piercing damage, mounting Strain generation, irreversible Calcification.

## 2. Class 1: The Vanguard (Warrior)
* **Narrative Identity:** Former garrison infantry and deserters from the city watch clad in dented, scavenged iron plate. Masters of narrow hallways, shield walls, and crushing posture breaks.

### Branch A: Cold Steel (The Iron Discipline)
* **T1 Active — Pommel Strike (2 AP | 0% Strain):** Heavy blunt strike with the hilt or crossguard. Deals moderate physical damage and **+20 Stagger**. Instantly knocks the target into a vulnerable Neutral Stance.
* **T1 Passive — Braced Poise:** While occupying Defensive Stance, gain **+15% Physical Mitigation** and total immunity to knockbacks, pulls, and forced movement from standard human enemies.
* **T2 Active — Intercepting Hook (3 AP | 0% Strain):** Step into an adjacent tile to intercept an incoming melee blow aimed at an ally. Reduces damage by current Armor Rating and pulls the attacker 1 tile closer.
* **T2 Passive — Stance Leverage:** Successfully mitigating or blocking an incoming attack refunds **1 AP** on the subsequent combat round (once per round limit).
* **T3 Active — Sunder & Pin (4 AP | 0% Strain):** Devastating downward billhook strike. Permanently tears off **3 Armor Rating** from the target and inflicts **Rooted** for 1 round.
* **Keystone — Unbroken Phalanx:** While remaining at 0% Strain, incoming enemy critical hits are downgraded to regular hits; active guard cannot be broken even by unblockable heavy blows.

### Branch B: Inoculated (The Marrow Graft)
* **T1 Active — Calcified Spike (1 AP | +15% Strain):** Serrated bone spurs punch violently through the vanguard's forearm flesh. Completely ignores target Armor Rating and inflicts **Bleed** (4 damage/round for 2 rounds).
* **T1 Passive — Marrow Chitin:** Permanently increases the hero's Dormant Strain Floor by **+10%**. Grants **+2 Base Armor Rating**, but consumable healing draughts are 20% less effective.
* **T2 Active — Bile Siphon (2 AP | +25% Strain):** Grafted glandular sacs exude caustic enzymes along the blade. Deals heavy corrosive damage and heals the vanguard for 50% of total damage dealt.
* **T2 Passive — Spasm Counter:** Upon crossing the threshold into Flesh-Tension (50%+ Strain), instantly deliver an automated bone-whip counter-attack against the last unit that attacked the vanguard.
* **T3 Active — Ribcage Lock (1 AP | +35% Strain):** The operative's internal ribcage expands outward through the chest plate, locking into an osseous carapace. Grants **+50% Mitigation** for 1 round. When struck, shatters outward in a 4-tile radial AoE bone-shrapnel burst.
* **Keystone — Aberrant Core:** Baseline Dormant Strain Floor permanently locks at 30%. While fighting in Aberrant Edge (75%–99% Strain), all active skills cost **-1 AP** (minimum 1 AP), but every completed battle permanently adds **+1 Calcification Point**.

## 3. Class 2: The Gutter-Stalker (Rogue)
* **Narrative Identity:** Rooftop runners, chimney sweeps, and curfew couriers. Specialized in slipping through quarantine grates, blind-spot assassinations, and shadow-phase infiltration.

### Branch A: Cold Steel (Cinder & Sinew)
* **T1 Active — Hamstring Sever (2 AP | 0% Strain):** Low slicing dagger strike. Lowers target Movement by **-2 tiles** for 2 rounds and disables enemy reaction attacks. Deals +40% bonus damage from behind.
* **T1 Passive — Prowler's Cadence:** Moving through low cover, darkness, or mist costs 0 bonus AP. Decreases the frontal detection cone of enemies by 2 tiles.
* **T2 Active — Ash-Cloud & Fade (3 AP | 0% Strain):** Smashes a pulverized chimney-soot flask against the stone. Creates a **3x3 smoke zone** that blocks all ranged targeting for 2 rounds; instantly disengages 2 tiles into stealth.
* **T2 Passive — Anatomical Flaw:** Precision attacks directed at Stunned, Staggered, or Rooted targets completely bypass Armor Rating and gain **+20% Critical Hit Chance**.
* **T3 Active — Throat-Carver's Art (4 AP | 0% Strain):** Unforgiving execution strike delivered from the rear arc. Deals massive piercing damage; instantly executes targets below 35% HP and refunds **2 AP**.
* **Keystone — Ghost of the Undercroft:** While at 0% Strain, backstab kills immediately re-enter stealth once per encounter and inflict permanent **Fear** on adjacent standard enemies.

### Branch B: Inoculated (The Umbral Graft)
* **T1 Active — Void-Flicker (1 AP | +15% Strain):** Operative dissolves their physical mass, instantly teleporting up to 3 tiles to an unoccupied square, ignoring all terrain obstacles, elevation, and enemy engagement zones.
* **T1 Passive — Viscous Shadow:** Baseline Dormant Strain Floor permanently increases by **+10%**. The operative's silhouette blurs: gains **+15% Evasion**, but light-based and phosphorus attacks deal +25% damage.
* **T2 Active — Flensing Filaments (2 AP | +25% Strain):** Shadowy tendons burst from fingertips up to range 4. Pulls the target 2 tiles, inflicts corrosive blight, and applies **Exposed** (-3 Armor Rating).
* **T2 Passive — Phase-Twitch:** While in Acute Resonance (25%–49% Strain), automatically phase-flicker 1 tile away before an incoming melee strike resolves (once per combat round limit).
* **T3 Active — Umbral Dissolution (2 AP | +35% Strain):** Dissolves physical form for 1 full round, granting total immunity to physical damage. Re-solidifying on the next turn grants +100% true eldritch damage on the next attack, but inflicts a 15% Max HP recoil on the stalker.
* **Keystone — Predatory Singularity:** Baseline Dormant Strain Floor permanently locks at 30%. While in Aberrant Edge, critical hits vent **20% Strain** and reset the Void-Flicker cooldown; finishing a battle in this state incurs **+1 Calcification Point**.

## 4. Class 3: The Arbalestier (Ranger — Bran)
* **Narrative Identity:** Tollhouse clocktower poachers and perimeter ridge scouts. Masters of long sightlines, heavy winch arbalests, wire trip-snares, and target suppression.

### Branch A: Cold Steel (The Iron Sight)
* **T1 Active — Notched Quarrel (2 AP | 0% Strain):** Measured arbalest release (range 4). High physical piercing damage and **+10 Stagger**. Ignores 1 point of Armor Rating.
* **T1 Passive — High-Ground Traverse:** When firing from elevation or rubble tiles, range increases by **+1 tile** and enemies suffer -10% hit chance against the arbalestier.
* **T2 Active — Tripwire Snare (3 AP | 0% Strain):** Deploys a coiled wire caltrop trap on an adjacent tile. The first enemy stepping onto the tile suffers 20 physical damage and is **Rooted** for 1 round.
* **T2 Passive — Cover Breaker:** Reduces target cover defense bonuses by **50%**; attacks directed against winding-up enemies gain **+25% Critical Hit Chance**.
* **T3 Active — Heavy Winch Sunder (4 AP | 0% Strain):** Heavy windup (T1–T4, impact T5). Knocks target back 2 tiles, shatters **3 Armor Rating**, deals colossal damage, and interrupts active windups.
* **Keystone — Dead-Eye Discipline:** While at 0% Strain, killing an enemy refunds **2 AP** and reveals all enemy planned movement paths for the next round.

### Branch B: Inoculated (The Ocular Graft)
* **T1 Active — Ocular Lock (1 AP | +15% Strain):** Grafted night-stalker pupil pulses crimson (range 5). Paints target: target cannot evade or enter stealth, and the arbalestier gains **+30% Critical Chance** against them for 2 rounds.
* **T1 Passive — Predatory Pupil:** Baseline Strain Floor increases by **+10%**. Ignores all darkness, fog, and smoke penalties; can target through low half-cover without penalty.
* **T2 Active — Splintering Chitin Bolt (2 AP | +25% Strain):** Fires an osseous spine bolt. Upon impact, splinters into jagged bone fragments dealing corrosive damage and **Bleed** (5 dmg/round for 2 rounds) to target and adjacent units.
* **T2 Passive — Reflex Twitch:** In Acute Resonance (25%–49% Strain), if targeted by an incoming ranged projectile during windup, automatically fire a free Snap interrupt bolt at the attacker (once per round).
* **T3 Active — Arterial Laser Pulse (2 AP | +35% Strain):** Channels raw eldritch energy through the ocular graft. Emits a continuous 6-tile laser beam that pierces through all units in a line, ignoring armor and barriers for massive true damage.
* **Keystone — Apex Stalker Core:** Baseline Strain Floor permanently locks at 30%. While in Aberrant Edge, all ranged attacks gain **100% Armor Piercing**, but battle completion in this state inflicts **+1 Calcification Point**.

## 5. Class 4: The Slag-Brute (Brawler — Torin)
* **Narrative Identity:** Escaped bone-mill laborers and foundry stokers. Masters of bare-handed brutality, improvised knuckle-guards, body throws, and structural demolition.

### Branch A: Cold Steel (The Forge Foundry)
* **T1 Active — Jaw-Breaker (2 AP | 0% Strain):** Crushing punch delivered with iron-shackled fists. Deals moderate damage and **+25 Stagger**. Knocks the target out of stance.
* **T1 Passive — Hypertrophy:** Increases Maximum HP by **+15%**; forced knockback distance inflicted on enemies increases by **+1 tile**.
* **T2 Active — Slag Slam & Throw (3 AP | 0% Strain):** Grabs an adjacent enemy and hurls them 2 tiles into another enemy or solid wall. Both entities suffer heavy blunt damage and are **Staggered**.
* **T2 Passive — Anvil Posture:** While in Neutral or Offensive stance, cannot be interrupted or staggered by Snap attacks; incoming melee blunt damage is reduced by **25%**.
* **T3 Active — Foundry Shatter (4 AP | 0% Strain):** Slams both fists into the flagstones. Destroys terrain tiles in a 3-tile frontal cone, converting them into rubble/hazard and knocking down all caught units (**Stunned** for 1 round).
* **Keystone — Unbroken Iron:** While at 0% Strain, dropping below 30% HP immediately refunds **2 AP** and grants **+4 Base Armor Rating** for 2 rounds.

### Branch B: Inoculated (The Marrow Titan)
* **T1 Active — Tendril Lash (1 AP | +15% Strain):** Thick muscle tendrils burst from forearms (range 2). Deals piercing damage and violently yanks the target into an adjacent tile.
* **T1 Passive — Osseous Bulk:** Baseline Strain Floor increases by **+10%**. Grants **+2 Base Armor Rating**, but movement speed is permanently reduced by **-1 tile**.
* **T2 Active — Boiling Slag Bile (2 AP | +25% Strain):** Vomits caustic, boiling mutagenic bile in a 3-tile line. Ignites the ground and strips **2 Armor Rating** per round for 2 rounds.
* **T2 Passive — Flesh Rampage:** In Flesh-Tension (50%+ Strain), every landed melee attack triggers an explosive radial bone shockwave hitting all adjacent foes for 15 damage.
* **T3 Active — Aberrant Colossus (2 AP | +35% Strain):** Biological mass violently doubles for 2 rounds. Grants total immunity to all crowd-control; melee attacks cleave 3 adjacent tiles for double damage, but unit suffers **15% Max HP recoil damage** per round.
* **Keystone — Juggernaut Core:** Baseline Strain Floor permanently locks at 30%. In Aberrant Edge, HP cannot drop below 1 (immortality for 1 round), but battle completion inflicts **+1 Calcification Point**.

## 6. Class 5: The Heretic Acolyte (Priest — Sister Maren)
* **Narrative Identity:** Defrocked church scribes and renegade embalmers. Masters of battlefield trauma medicine, posture restoration, and aberrant marrow strain siphoning.

### Branch A: Cold Steel (The Field Suture)
* **T1 Active — Brimstone Cauterize (2 AP | 0% Strain):** Cleanses Bleed, Poison, or Burn from an ally within 2 tiles and restores **35 HP**.
* **T1 Passive — Anatomical Precision:** Allies standing within 2 tiles of Maren gain **+10% Stance Stability** and **+10% Debuff Resistance**.
* **T2 Active — Tourniquet & Tonic (3 AP | 0% Strain):** Rushes to a heavily wounded ally (range 2). Heals **75 HP**, restores broken stances to Neutral, and grants **Haste (+1 Move)** for 1 round.
* **T2 Passive — Triage Efficiency:** Delivering healing skills to an ally below 30% HP immediately refunds **1 AP** (once per round).
* **T3 Active — Resilience Ward (4 AP | 0% Strain):** Chants ancient pre-cataclysm liturgical verses while swinging bronze censer. Grants entire 4-man squad **+30% Physical Mitigation** and **Stagger Immunity** for 1 round.
* **Keystone — Pure Hippocratic Oath:** While at 0% Strain, all sutures and field draughts heal **40% more HP**; Maren cannot be critically hit by enemies.

### Branch B: Inoculated (The Marrow Siphon)
* **T1 Active — Strain Siphon (1 AP | +15% Strain):** Extends blackened veins across an ally (range 3). Siphons up to **30% Strain** away from the ally into Maren's own gauge, healing the ally for **50 HP**.
* **T1 Passive — Sympathetic Marrow:** Baseline Strain Floor increases by **+10%**. When allies within 3 tiles take damage, Maren absorbs +5% Strain but grants the ally **+2 Armor Rating**.
* **T2 Active — Ichor Thread Suture (2 AP | +25% Strain):** Launches living black ichor threads to stitch catastrophic wounds (range 3). Heals **120 HP**, but increases target ally's Strain by **+10%**.
* **T2 Passive — Resonant Catharsis:** When Maren crosses into Flesh-Tension (50%+ Strain), all allies within 3 tiles immediately vent **15% Strain**.
* **T3 Active — Aberrant Communion (2 AP | +35% Strain):** Psychically links the circulatory systems of the entire squad for 1 round. All damage taken by any ally is pooled and halved; Maren's single-target heals apply to all squad members simultaneously.
* **Keystone — Martyr's Singularity:** Baseline Strain Floor permanently locks at 30%. While in Aberrant Edge, if an ally would take fatal damage, Maren absorbs the blow entirely; battle completion in this state inflicts **+1 Calcification Point**.

## 7. Class 6: The Ichor Alchemist (Mage — Elyse)
* **Narrative Identity:** Tenement hedge occultists and outlaw distillers. Masters of phosphorus fire, blinding flash-pots, caustic acid sprays, and subterranean miasma.

### Branch A: Cold Steel (Volatile Pyrotechnics)
* **T1 Active — Phosphorus Pot (2 AP | 0% Strain):** Throws a volatile burning flask (range 4). Creates a **3x3 burning ground zone** for 2 rounds, dealing fire damage to any unit entering or ending turn in it.
* **T1 Passive — Combustion Formula:** All thrown chemical items and fire skills deal **+20% bonus damage** against enemies standing in tar, oil, or dry timber.
* **T2 Active — Magnesium Flash-Bomb (3 AP | 0% Strain):** Detonates a blinding flash-powder canister in a 3x3 radius. Inflicts **Blind** (cannot target beyond 1 tile) and **+20 Stagger** on all caught enemies.
* **T2 Passive — Catalytic Munitions:** Enemies afflicted by Burning or Blind have their Armor Rating permanently reduced by **-2**.
* **T3 Active — Suffocating Choke-Gas (4 AP | 0% Strain):** Deploys a dense 4x4 cloud of soot and brimstone. Blocks all ranged line-of-sight; enemies trapped inside lose **1 AP per round** and cannot cast spells.
* **Keystone — Master Alchemist:** While at 0% Strain, all thrown flasks and grenades gain **+1 tile AoE radius** and cost **-1 AP** (minimum 1 AP).

### Branch B: Inoculated (The Mutagenic Catalyst)
* **T1 Active — Caustic Vitriol (1 AP | +15% Strain):** Hurls raw aberrant digestive enzymes (range 4). Ignores armor completely, applies **Exposed (-4 Armor)**, and inflicts corrosive decay for 3 rounds.
* **T1 Passive — Toxic Blood:** Baseline Strain Floor increases by **+10%**. When Elyse is struck by melee attacks, the attacker suffers **15 corrosive recoil damage** and is splashed with acid.
* **T2 Active — Miasma of the Low Sleep (2 AP | +25% Strain):** Vents a swirling violet cloud of subterranean spore-gas in a 3x3 zone (range 4). Enemies inside suffer eldritch hallucinations (50% chance to attack nearest ally) and take **+5% Strain per tick**.
* **T2 Passive — Aberrant Catalysis:** In Flesh-Tension (50%+ Strain), all corrosive damage dealt by Elyse's skills heals her for **25% of damage dealt**.
* **T3 Active — Liquefaction Transmutation (2 AP | +35% Strain):** Channels the dreaming titan's tremors, transmuting a 3x3 stone floor area into bubbling tar-bog. Ensnared enemies are **Rooted** and suffer continuous liquefaction acid damage.
* **Keystone — Primordial Core:** Baseline Strain Floor permanently locks at 30%. In Aberrant Edge, Elyse's acid skills permanently dissolve enemy equipment; battle completion in this state inflicts **+1 Calcification Point**.

## 8. Starter Squad Roster & Visual Briefs (The Six Founders)

When starting a campaign, the initial four-man cell of The Severed Root consists of:

1. **Kaelen (The Vanguard):**
   - *Appearance:* Battered kettle helm, defaced breastplate with a gouged-out cathedral cross, notched billhook poleaxe.
   - *Taint:* His left arm is wrapped in bloody linen rags; beneath them peek jagged, calcified white bone spurs.
2. **Vesper (The Gutter-Stalker):**
   - *Appearance:* Shredded soot-stained cloth cowl, slate-shingle leather jerkin, twin inverted bodkin daggers.
   - *Taint:* Her fingertips dissolve into a continuous shifting black smoke mist; her cast shadow points unnaturally toward enemies regardless of light sources.
3. **Maren (The Heretic Acolyte):**
   - *Appearance:* Frayed white cleric habit worn underneath a stained leather butcher's apron, cracked round spectacles, rusty bone-saw and a bronze censer staff.
   - *Taint:* Blackened, pulsing veins creep up her throat and across her jawline.
4. **Bran (The Arbalestier):**
   - *Appearance:* Hunting brigandine with a thick horn recoil pauldron, oversized lever-action arbalest.
   - *Taint:* A grafted vertical night-stalker pupil in his left eye that glows with a faint arterial red targeting beam when taking aim.
