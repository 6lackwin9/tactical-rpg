## 1. Executive Summary & Feedback

The additions of an **episodic introductory prologue**, **mid-campaign authored recruits/guests**, and **high-calcification "Living Martyrs"** significantly elevate both narrative attachment and mechanical depth:

1. **The Episodic Prologue:** Replacing a sterile menu start with an interactive, sequential prologue introduces the 4 founders organically, teaching mechanics (stances, backstabs, triage, ranged pins) in bite-sized encounters.
2. **Mid-Campaign Named Figures & Safehouse Guests:** Solves the coldness of purely procedural recruits. Rescuing key named figures who wait in the safehouse as guests before taking a bunk gives the player meaningful personnel decisions.
3. **High-Calcification Disposable Weapons ("The Broken Martyrs"):** A masterstroke of grimdark ludonarrative design. Liberating dying, heavily mutated prisoners at Stage III or IV Calcification to use as short-lived, terrifying siege weapons turns corruption into an active tactical asset with profound ethical weight.

## 2. The 3-Step Interactive Prologue: "The Convergence in the Gutters"

Before Mission 1 of Phase 1 begins, the player plays through a short, linked narrative prologue that introduces the four founders and teaches the core combat engine:

```text
PROLOGUE PROGRESSION:
[ STEP 1: KAELEN'S FLIGHT ] ===> [ STEP 2: VESPER'S AMBUSH ] ===> [ STEP 3: THE MORTUARY CART ] ===> [ THE SLUICE UNDERCROFT ]
Controls: Vanguard Solo         Controls: Vanguard + Stalker     Controls: Full 4-Man Squad           Safehouse Reached!
Teaches: Poise, Guard, Windup    Teaches: Flanking, Ash, Stepping  Teaches: Arbalest Pins, Triage        Act I Mission 1 Begins
```

### 2.1 Step 1: Kaelen's Flight (The Deserter)
* **Setting:** An ash-slick alleyway behind the Seventh Ward garrison during a torrential downpour.
* **Narrative:** Kaelen has just refused an order to club screaming youths into a culling wagon, striking down his squad corporal. Two Castellan guards corner him.
* **Tutorial Mechanics:** Teaches basic 6-tick timeline windups, Armor Rating absorption, and using *Pommel Strike* to Stagger an attacking foe out of their windup.

### 2.2 Step 2: Vesper's Ambush (The Courier)
* **Setting:** The rotting shingle rooftops and narrow overhangs above the Meat Market.
* **Narrative:** As reinforcements corner Kaelen, Vesper drops from a broken flue, hurling an ash flask into the inquisitors' eyes.
* **Tutorial Mechanics:** Introduces the Gutter-Stalker; teaches directional facing, backstab criticals, using *Hamstring Sever* to lock enemy movement, and smoke zone cover.

### 2.3 Step 3: The Mortuary Cart (The Embalmer & The Poacher)
* **Setting:** A barred canal bridge where an iron mortuary cage wagon is stranded under watchtower crosshairs.
* **Narrative:** Sister Maren and Bran are chained inside the cart, awaiting transfer to the dissection crypts. Kaelen and Vesper assault the bridge guards to free them.
* **Tutorial Mechanics:** Introduces Bran's ranged line-of-sight targeting and *Pinning Shot*; teaches Maren's *Field Surgery* and stance restoration.
* **Prologue Resolution:** With alarms echoing across Cinderreach, Vesper wrenches open a submerged drainage grate. The four leap into the rushing black runoff, washing up directly in **The Sluice Undercroft**, where Bram Karr and Sister Aline are waiting.

## 3. Mid-Campaign Authored Recruits & Safehouse Guests

Roster expansion is not limited to generic procedural youths. Throughout Phases 1, 2, and 3, major questlines and side investigations unlock **Authored Secondary Recruits**:

```text
                           [ THE RESCUE ENCOUNTER ]
                                      |
                                      v
                        [ THE SAFEHOUSE CENTRAL BASIN ]
                        (Safehouse Guest / Observer)
                        - Provides Safehouse Facility Perk
                        - Does not consume a Bunk
                                      |
                       [ BUNK VACANCY OPENS IN HOLLOWS ]
                                      |
                                      v
                       [ OFFICIALLY SWORN INTO ROSTER ]
                        (Active Combat Operative)
```

### 3.1 The "Safehouse Guest" Mechanic
* When a named NPC is rescued, they are escorted to **The Sluice Undercroft** as a **Guest**.
* **Zero Bunk Cost Initially:** They sleep in the communal Central Basin. They do not take up one of the 6–8 precious Dry Hollows bunks.
* **Safehouse Perks:** While in guest status, their presence grants a passive benefit to the hideout:
  - *Example 1: Orin the Clock-Smith:* Rescued in Phase 2. While a guest, grants $+10\%$ speed to Bram's armor re-tempering.
  - *Example 2: Sister Theresa (Heretic Herbalist):* Rescued in Phase 2. Lowers the salt cost of Aline's chemical strain flushes by $20\%$.
* **Swearing In:** Whenever a bunk opens up (due to hero death, retirement, or safehouse upgrade), the player can speak with any Guest in the basin to formally induct them into the active combat roster.

## 4. The Broken Martyrs: High-Calcification Temporary Operatives

Inquisitor torture cells, church flesh-pens, and bone-mills hold prisoners who have been subjected to forced mutagenic experiments or prolonged blight mist exposure. They are known as **The Living Martyrs** or **The Marrow-Broken**.

```text
[ RESCUE LIVING MARTYR ] ===> [ ENLIST FOR 1–2 SUICIDAL STRIKES ] ===> [ TERMINAL DRIFT / SACRIFICE ] ===> [ HARVEST APEX GRAFT ]
Starts at Stage III/IV        Terrifying transhuman burst;            Body collapses; ego dissolves;        Leaves rare mutated organ
Calcification; dying body.     immune to fear; true armor pierce.      safehouse honors their memory.        for long-term squad upgrades!
```

### 4.1 Narrative Concept & Moral Weight
These are tragic, dying souls whose humanity is nearly spent. Their bodies are cracked with osseous chitin plates; black blood drips from their tear ducts. They cannot be saved or cleansed—their Calcification Clock is already at **Stage III or Stage IV**.
- They know they have days or hours before total mental dissolution.
- They offer their remaining breath as living battering rams against the regime that mutilated them.
- *The Ethical Dilemma:* Does the player unleash a dying human as a biological weapon, or grant them a peaceful bed to pass away in?

### 4.2 Gameplay & Combat Mechanics of Martyrs
* **Overwhelming Transhuman Power:**
  - They arrive with **Tier III Apex Grafts** pre-installed.
  - They possess natural **Armor Piercing** on all strikes, innate **Fear Aura** debuffing adjacent enemies, and $+50\%$ damage bonuses.
* **The Rapid Collapse Clock:**
  - They start permanently at **Stage III or Stage IV Calcification**.
  - Every combat encounter they participate in automatically advances their Calcification by **+1 Stage**.
  - After 1 or 2 missions, they inevitably reach **Stage V (Terminal Drift)** and collapse.
* **The Legacy Harvest:**
  - When a Living Martyr falls or dissolves into Terminal Drift, their mutated organ does not rot. Sister Aline Vance can harvest it at the slab, yielding a **Rare Apex Graft** that can be permanently installed into your long-term recruits. Their sacrifice fuels the permanent strength of the cell.

| Recruit Category | Recruitment Source | Bunk Requirement | Lifespan / Durability | Narrative Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Founding Four** | Episodic Prologue | Permanent (4 bunks) | Long / Indefinite (Phase 1 pure; 1 scripted loss in Climax) | Core emotional anchors; personal questlines. |
| **Rescued Named Figures** | Major Story Milestones | Safehouse Guest first; takes bunk on induction | Standard Roster Lifespan (Governed by Calcification Clock) | Unique dialogue, authored perks, faction ties. |
| **The Unsealed Pool** | Culling Wagon Raids | Requires free bunk | Standard Roster Lifespan | Procedural reinforcement soldiers; origin traits. |
| **The Living Martyrs** | Inquisitor Death-Pens | Quarantine Crypt (0 bunks) | **1 to 2 Missions Max** (Starts at Stage III/IV Calcification) | High-burst suicide shock-troops; leaves Apex Graft upon death. |
