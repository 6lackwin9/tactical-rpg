## 1. Executive Summary & Design Validation

The integration of **all six foundational classes as authored Phase 1 founders** perfectly aligns with the Sluice Undercroft's **initial 6-bunk capacity**. 

This structure creates a deliberate three-act gameplay evolution:
1. **Phase 1 (The Iron Vanguard):** Master the 6 classes through pure Cold Steel. Inoculation exists only in controlled, narrative teasers that showcase its terrifying power and biological cost.
2. **Phase 2 (The Transhuman Rupture):** The player chooses their operational philosophy: either surrender to corruption and treat the roster as disposable living weapons, or carefully balance Cold Steel with minimal grafts to preserve the sanity and humanity of their veterans.
3. **Phase 3 (The Blight of the Low Sleep):** Corruption leaks beyond combat into safehouse survival. The sleeping titan stirs; cathedral bells induce resonance tremors; warded tallow shortages cause nightmares; and high-calcification veterans require psychological quarantine.

### 2.1 The Two New Phase 1 Founders

#### Torin "Slag-Jaw" (The Slag-Brute / Brawler)
* **Age:** 22  
* **Visual Appearance:** Towering, soot-stained youth with heavy iron shackles still riveted around his scarred wrists (used as improvised knuckle-guards). Half his jaw is burned from molten slag splashback.
* **Backstory:** Worked 14-hour shifts in the Fourth Kiln bone-mills under Foundry-Master Vane, pulverizing calcified corpses for masonry mortar. Escaped after cracking an overseer's skull with a shovel.
* **Recruitment Mission (*"The Bone-Mill Raid"*):** The cell ambushes a night transport cart carrying bone-mill defectors to the Crucible furnace. Breaking Torin's cage introduces brawler grappling and close-range environmental knockbacks.

#### Elyse (The Ichor Alchemist / Mage)
* **Age:** 19  
* **Visual Appearance:** Slight frame draped in an oversized, patched leather apothecary apron stained with copper-green vitriol; cracked brass goggles perched on her forehead. A wicker bandolier holds volatile glass flasks of sulfur and white phosphorus.
* **Backstory:** Ran an underground chemical dispensary beneath the Ash-Crawl Tenements, brewing counterfeit tallow and anti-sedative tonics for commoners until an Inquisitorial purge team torched her cellar.
* **Recruitment Mission (*"The Cellar Purge"*):** The squad navigates a burning tenement block to extract Elyse before cathedral incinerator squads seal the vents. Introduces thrown alchemical flash-pots and line-of-sight choke smoke.

## 3. Phase 1: Pure Cold Steel & Controlled Inoculation Teasers

Throughout Phase 1, the player relies almost entirely on physical discipline, biomechanical leverage, and stance management. However, the game foreshadows Inoculation through **scripted narrative teasers**:

1. **The Inquisitor's Secret:** During the rescue of Torin, the squad defeats an Inquisitor Sergeant whose breastplate cracks open, revealing a pulsating, grafted aberration gland that rapidly knits his wounds until severed. The founders realize the regime's elite are hypocrites who harvest the very mutations they execute commoners for.
2. **Sister Aline's Emergency Syringe (Narrative Combat Teaser):**
   - In Mission 5, against an elite iron-plated Castellan who cannot be pierced by standard weapons, Sister Aline hands the player **one sealed dose of raw extracted marrow**.
   - The player can inject it into an operative for 1 round: the operative instantly manifests aberrant power (gaining $+100\%$ true damage and ignoring armor), shattering the boss.
   - **The Dramatic Cost:** The moment the battle ends, the injected hero violently collapses, coughing black bile, permanently blackening the veins along their neck (**Stage I Calcification**). The squad witnesses firsthand the terrifying bargain of the marrow.

## 4. Phase 2: The Two Strategic Philosophies (Disposable vs. Preserved)

With the death of one of the six founders at the Crucible climax and the unlocking of Sister Aline's **Apothecary Slab**, Phase 2 forces the player to adopt a distinct operational philosophy:

```text
                                  [ THE PHASE 2 DIVERGENCE ]
                                               |
             +---------------------------------+---------------------------------+
             |                                                                   |
             v                                                                   v
[ THE FLESH-BROKER PHILOSOPHY ]                                   [ THE PRESERVATIONIST PHILOSOPHY ]
"The Cause Demands Power"                                         "Hold the Human Line"
- Full transition into Inoculation                                - Minimal, tactical hybrid grafting
- Pushes deep into Aberrant Edge (75%–99%)                        - Strict "Strain Surfing" capped at 49%
- Treats procedural recruits as disposable shock troops           - Relies heavily on Bram's masterwork forge
- Bypasses armor and crushes bosses in 2 rounds                   - Slower, tactical attrition combat
- High turnover: units calcify, retire, and are replaced          - Veterans survive with minds and bodies intact
```

### Dynamic Ludonarrative Tension
* Neither path is "wrong"—the game supports both:
  - The **Flesh-Broker** experiences explosive, visceral power and flashy screen-clearing maneuvers, but constantly manages an unstable, mutating roster on the brink of collapse.
  - The **Preservationist** experiences intense, high-friction tactical chess where every inch of positioning matters, preserving their beloved founding heroes through sheer mechanical skill.

## 5. Phase 3: The Macro-Corruption & Sanity Management System

In Phase 3, the existential horror shifts from an in-combat tactical threat to an omnipresent environmental siege. The slumbering leviathan (**The Low Sleep**) begins to stir beneath Cinderreach, causing corruption to leak directly into safehouse survival:

### 5.1 The Continental Resonance (Bell Tremors)
* High Cathedral bells toll on an accelerating world clock.
* Every time a major bell cycle tolls, any operative in the roster who possesses **$\ge 1$ point of Calcification** feels their petrified marrow vibrate in sympathy with the titan.
* Operatives outside safehouse warded light gain **passive out-of-combat Strain**, pushing them closer to spontaneous psychosis.

### 5.2 Warded Tallow Depletion & Nightmare Psychosis
* In Phase 3, warded tallow becomes dangerously scarce as the Tallow Guild locks down city pipelines.
* If safehouse tallow reserves drop below the daily upkeep threshold:
  - The protective yellowish-green flame in the Dry Hollows flickers out.
  - Subterranean mist-aberrations seep into the sleeping vaults.
  - Idle reserve operatives suffer **"The Leviathan's Whispers"**, losing Composure and developing severe mental neuroses (e.g., *Paranoid Insomnia*, *Terrified Claustrophobia*, *Homicidal Twitching*).

### 5.3 The Quarantine Crypts in The Dry Hollows
* Senior operatives who reach **Stage IV Calcification ("The Shakes")** emit an audible, crystalline hum from their hardened bones.
* If left in communal bunks, they panic younger recruits, reducing their starting AP on missions.
* The player must manage **Isolation Quarantines**: placing Stage IV heroes into sealed brick kiln crypts and expending precious brimstone/salt flushes at Sister Aline's slab to stabilize their minds before deploying them on final suicide strikes against the cathedral bastions.
