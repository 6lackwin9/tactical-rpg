## 1. Narrative Architecture: Establishing the Four Before Act 1

To ensure the player is deeply invested in the initial squad before the campaign opens, the prologue is structured not merely as a combat tutorial, but as **four interconnected narrative vignettes** that reveal:
1. Who each character was within the oppressive feudal machinery.
2. The exact breaking point / trauma that forced them to rebel.
3. Their secret physical mutation (ambient eldritch taint).
4. The dramatic fireside dialogue at **The Sluice Undercroft** where their fragile alliance is forged before Act 1 Mission 1 begins.


## 2. The Four Founding Dossiers & Prologue Vignettes

---

### 2.1 Kaelen (The Vanguard — Age 21)
* **Pre-War Social Role:** Junior Enlisted Guardsman, *Seventh Ward Castellan Watch*.
* **Visual Identity:** Battered kettle helm, dented iron breastplate with the cathedral cross violently gouged out with a chisel, notched billhook poleaxe. Left arm bound tightly in blood-soaked linen rags.
* **The Backstory & Breaking Point:**
  - Born to an impoverished tailor in the Lower Tenements. He took the Castellan oath at age 17 simply to secure the daily loaf of salt-bread and warded tallow candle for his ailing mother.
  - For four years, Kaelen stood guard during curfew, telling himself that maintaining order kept the sector safe from the horrors of the Shroud.
  - **The Trauma:** During the dawn sweep for the upcoming Red Tithing, his squad was ordered to breach a neighborhood cellar and drag sixteen adolescent apprentices into iron cage wagons. When a 15-year-old girl—the daughter of his mother's neighbor—screamed and clung to his boots, his squad corporal, Sergeant Vane, ordered Kaelen to shatter her knees with his billhook pommel. Kaelen snapped: he turned his billhook on the corporal, broke his jaw, kicked over the lantern, and screamed for the children to run into the dark.
* **His Eldritch Taint:** Beneath the linen rags on his left forearm, calcified white bone spurs have begun punching through his skin—a result of breathing unwarded mist while standing night watches on the outer ramparts. He knows that if garrison surgeons inspect him, he will be vivisected as a mutant.
* **In-Prologue Gameplay:** Introduces single-unit tactical control: Braced Guard, Armor absorption, and using *Pommel Strike* on Windup Ticks to Stagger attacking guards.

---

### 2.2 Vesper (The Gutter-Stalker — Age 18)
* **Pre-War Social Role:** Curfew Courier & Rooftop Runner, *Ash-Crawl Tenements*.
* **Visual Identity:** Shredded charcoal cowl, slate-shingle leather jerkin, twin inverted matte-black bodkin daggers. Smears chimney soot across her cheeks.
* **The Backstory & Breaking Point:**
  - An orphan whose parents were spent bone-mill laborers who succumbed to black-lung ossification when she was nine. Vesper grew up in the dizzying heights between gargantuan basalt cathedral spires and crumbling tenement roofs.
  - She became an indispensable runner for the underground black market, carrying coded bone-ciphers, warded sulfur matches, and medicine across the city, sliding past searchlight beams.
  - **The Trauma:** Her courier cell was betrayed by an informant in exchange for two bags of rock-salt. She returned to her tenement roost to find three inquisitorial purgers hanging her fellow couriers from the chimney flues with iron wire. Armed only with her bodkins and ash flasks, she slipped into the shadows, vowing to gut every masked inquisitor in the ward.
* **His Eldritch Taint:** Her fingertips dissolve into a continuous, shifting black mist that wafts upward like cold smoke. When she focuses, her cast shadow detaches from light sources, stretching unnaturally toward nearby enemies.
* **In-Prologue Gameplay:** Introduces flanking mechanics: directional backstabs, throwing ash-flasks to create sight-blocking 3x3 smoke zones, and using *Hamstring Sever* to immobilize fleeing enemies.

---

### 2.3 Sister Maren (The Heretic Acolyte — Age 23)
* **Pre-War Social Role:** Prosector's Scribe, *The Sovereign Clerisy's Hall of Holy Dissection*.
* **Visual Identity:** Stained white cleric habit worn beneath a heavy, blood-crusted leather butcher's apron; cracked round spectacles; wields a rusted bone-saw and a bronze censer crook staff.
* **The Backstory & Breaking Point:**
  - An elite scholar who spent six years transcribing Latinate medical texts and cataloging physiological mutations in the deep cathedral crypts. She genuinely believed church dogma—that the Red Tithing was a holy, sorrowful necessity that pacified the dreaming titan to preserve mankind.
  - **The Trauma:** While auditing the Clerisy's underground apothecary ledgers, Maren discovered the **"Doctrine of Stagnation"**: confidential orders proving that the Clerisy was intentionally lacing commoner water cisterns and communion draughts with crushed bone sediment. The Church was deliberately inducing premature calcification and mental docility in all citizens before age 25 to prevent rebellion.
  - When Maren attempted to smuggle out the ledger fragments, the Arch-Prelate defrocked her, branded her throat with an iron sigil, and ordered her transferred to the vivisection cart.
* **Her Eldritch Taint:** Blackened, pulsing veins creep up her throat and across her jawline, caused by years of handling raw aberration organs without protective beeswax gloves. She can sense eldritch resonance through her teeth.
* **In-Prologue Gameplay:** Introduces support and recovery: battlefield sutures, clearing enemy Stagger debuffs, and using her censer staff to restore stance poise.

---

### 2.4 Bran (The Arbalestier — Age 20)
* **Pre-War Social Role:** Clocktower Toll-Poacher & Perimeter Scout.
* **Visual Identity:** Stiff hunting brigandine with a heavy horn recoil pauldron on his right shoulder; oversized lever-action steel arbalest; leather quiver of notched bone quarrels.
* **The Backstory & Breaking Point:**
  - Raised in the misty limestone crags outside Cinderreach where his family survived by hunting feral ridge-hounds and scavenging pre-cataclysm iron scraps.
  - Masters of extreme-range marksmanship, Bran and his younger sister, Lyda, poached church supply wagons that crossed the mountain passes.
  - **The Trauma:** An inquisitor culling detachment surrounded their homestead at midnight. Rather than surrender his fourteen-year-old sister to the culling cages, Bran climbed into the roof rafters with his arbalest. When the Castellan commander dragged Lyda into the yard, Bran put a heavy iron quarrel straight through the commander's throat, buying Lyda time to vanish into the foggy crags. Out of bolts, Bran was dragged down by armored guards, beaten with lead saps, and locked in the mortuary wagon in heavy chains.
* **His Eldritch Taint:** A grafted night-stalker vertical pupil in his left eye—a self-inflicted surgical mutilation he carved into himself using a sharpened silver cannula and the optic nerve of a slain cave-beast to aim through the pitch-black gloom of the Shroud. When aiming, his pupil emits a thin, arterial-red targeting beam across the grid.
* **In-Prologue Gameplay:** Introduces ranged line-of-sight: piercing distance attacks, calculating cover percentages, and using *Pinning Shot* to root heavy enemies in place before they reach melee range.

## 3. Step 3 of the Prologue: The Mortuary Cart Convergence

The third playable encounter of the prologue is the dramatic convergence where the four meet under combat conditions:

```text
SCENE: THE CANAL BRIDGE CHOKEPOINT — THE MORTUARY WAGON
```

* **The Setup:** A heavy iron-barred mortuary wagon is stuck at the apex of a narrow stone bridge over the sewer canal. Maren and Bran are chained inside; three Inquisitor guards and a crossbowman hold the far side.
* **The Encounter Flow:**
  1. Kaelen and Vesper attack the bridge from the rear.
  2. Vesper drops an ash cloud over the crossbowman; Kaelen sunder-strikes the iron padlock off the wagon cage.
  3. Maren and Bran burst out: Bran snatches an arbalest from a fallen rack, while Maren grabs an embalmer's bone-saw and censer.
  4. For the first time, the player commands the **full four-person cell**, coordinating frontline tanking, flanking, ranged pins, and field sutures.
* **The Escape:** Cathedral sirens wail. Watchtower searchlights sweep the stone. Vesper jams an iron crowbar into the drowned drainage grate at the bridge foot, levering the iron bars aside:
  > *"Down! If they catch us on the cobblestones, we're ash!"*
  The four leap into the rushing black runoff water and vanish beneath the city.

## 4. The Respite at the Hearth: Safehouse Induction Beat

Before Act 1 Mission 1 is unlocked, the game transitions into an intimate, quiet cinematic dialogue scene in **The Sluice Undercroft**:

```text
LOCATION: THE SLUICE UNDERCROFT — THE CENTRAL BASIN & TINKER'S HEARTH
```

The camera pans across the subterranean redoubt. Water drips steadily into the central pool. A dull orange glow emanates from **Bram Karr's ventless charcoal forge**. At the stone dissection slab, **Sister Aline Vance** wipes black ichor from her scalpels.

The four wash up on the flagstones, bruised, soaked, and bleeding.

### The Fireside Interaction:
* **Kaelen** slumps onto a stone bench, gritting his teeth as he strips the soaked linen from his left arm. The white bone spurs gleam in the forge firelight.
* **Sister Maren** kneels beside him, spectacles perched on her nose. Her fingers trace the jagged osteocytes with professional dread:
  > **Maren:** *"Chitinous ossification. Third metatarsal branch. You've been breathing unwarded mist for six months, haven't you?"*  
  > **Kaelen:** *"Stood watch on the North Curtain. The lanterns went dry in the third watch. How long do I have, Sister?"*  
  > **Maren:** *"Four months before the calcium locks your elbow into stone. Six before it reaches your chest. Unless the bells wake it sooner."*
* **Vesper** huddles near the forge coals, wringing black soot from her hair, bodkins resting across her knees:
  > **Vesper:** *"We don't have four months, soldier. The Red Tithing is in six weeks. The watch is sealing every surface grate in the ward. If we sit down here in the dark, the Inquisitors will flush the drains with quicklime. If we go up, we need steel, salt, and targets."*
* **Bran** sits on an upturned nail keg, methodically checking the winch cable of his arbalest. His left eye glows like a red ember in the gloom:
  > **Bran:** *"Steel I can use. Plans are for people who intend to grow old. None of us in this room are ever going to see five-and-twenty. So let's crack as many iron masks as we can before the stone takes us."*
* **Bram "Iron-Lung" Karr** slams a flanged hammer onto his anvil, throwing orange sparks into the shadows:
  > **Bram:** *"Enough talk. You survived the street sweep. That makes you either very lucky or very dangerous. There are four dry bunks in the Hollows behind me. Bread and salt on the shelf. Sleep while you can, young blood. When the next cathedral bell tolls, the war begins."*

The screen fades gently. **Act I: The Sunk Ward** appears on screen. The safehouse UI unlocks with Kaelen, Vesper, Maren, and Bran assigned to four bunks, ready for their first deployment.
