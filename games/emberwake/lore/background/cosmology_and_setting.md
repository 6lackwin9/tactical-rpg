## 1. The High Concept

*Emberwake* takes place in a decaying, ash-choked medieval realm where human civilization has been subjugated by the **Sovereign Clerisy**—an ancient ecclesiastical aristocracy that maintains total dominion through feudal hierarchies and gruesome blood-pacts with an incomprehensible entity slumbering directly beneath the continental crust.

The natural order of life, death, light, and seasons has completely shattered. The world does not experience sunlight, seasonal rebirth, or natural organic decay. The living exist on borrowed time, caught between the suffocating labor demands of the cathedral factories and the physiological horror of subterranean mutations.

## 2. Broken Laws of Nature

### 2.1 The Shroud (The False Heavens)
Two centuries ago, an event known colloquially as **The Extinguishing** snuffed out the stars, moon, and sun. 
- In their place hangs **The Shroud**: a permanent, bruised-violet canopy of petrified cloud, freezing gloom, and particulate ash drifting from highland cathedral kilns.
- Day and night no longer cycle naturally. Temporal life, labor shifts, curfew hours, and church tithes are synchronized across the settlements solely by the deep, resonant tolling of the Hegemony's massive, warded cathedral bells.
- True celestial light is an extinct memory; illumination exists only via torchlight, tallow fires, or sickly phosphorescent fungal growths from the catacombs.

### 2.2 The Low Sleep (The Subterranean Leviathan)
The landmass rests directly upon the calcified flesh and slumbering nervous system of an ancient, cosmic leviathan referred to in heretical texts as **The Low Sleep** or **The Slumbering Root**.
- The entity is not dead; it breathes and dreams.
- Its sluggish subterranean pulse induces periodic seismic tremors, sudden ground liquefaction (creating boiling "tar-bogs" that swallow entire streets), and the upward seepage of mutagenic black marrow into wells, coal seams, and subterranean sewers.
- Proximity to these seepages warps flesh and distorts space, turning deep mining tunnels into shifting, non-Euclidean labyrinths.

### 2.3 The Stagnation of Mortality (Broken Decomposition)
Natural biological rot is broken. Organic matter does not decompose into fertile soil or loam:
- **Ossification:** Uncremated flesh dries, blackens, and rapidly ossifies into hard, brittle black bone.
- **The Skinless Wanderers:** A corpse buried without proper rites does not dissolve; the ambient marrow of the Low Sleep seeps into the sinew, knitting the muscle and tendon back together over weeks into mindless, skinless husks driven by blind subterranean urges.
- **The Salt & Sulfur Rite:** The only permanent disposal of the deceased requires exhaustive cremation with sanctified rock salt, crushed brimstone, and warded tallow oils. Because salt and fuel are strictly rationed by the Clerisy, poor families are frequently forced to sell the corpses of loved ones to the **Tallow Guild** or **Bone-Mills** to pay off ancestral debts.

## 3. The Generational Divide

Ambient eldritch exposure and church-administered sedatives have split the human population into two distinct biological castes based on age.

```
AGE SPECTRUM:
[ 0 ------------ 14 ]      [ 15 ------------ 24 ]      [ 25 --------------------- 50+ ]
   The Caged Young            The Unsealed               The Spent Generation
   (Hostages & Pens)          (The Player's Pool)        (Docile, Calcified, Addicted)
```

### 3.1 The Spent Generation (Ages 25+)
Decades of inhaling warded tallow fumes, working the dust-choked bone-mills, and drinking church-issued communion draughts (laced with mild ossifying sedatives to suppress dissent) leave older humans mentally docile and physically rigid:
- **Physical Traits:** Chalky, stiffened joints, clouded cataracts, brittle skin, and shallow, wheezing breath.
- **Psychological Compliance:** Their fear centers are deadened, replaced by a desperate dependence on cathedral warmth and bell-schedules.
- **Societal Role:** The spent populace actively polices their own children, reporting runaways and heretics to the Iron Inquisition in exchange for extra salt vouchers, tallow candles, and safety from the culling wagons.

### 3.2 The Unsealed (Ages 15–24)
The youth of Emberwake represent the only supple, biologically vibrant segment of humanity:
- **Physiological Resilience:** Their circulatory systems remain elastic, allowing them to exert peak physical power and witness cosmic warping or aberration forms without lapsing into immediate vegetative catatonia.
- **The Ticking Clock:** Adulthood carries an inevitable, terrifying countdown. By age 25, the ambient marrow and environmental blight catch up with them. If they survive the Inquisition's press-gangs and holding pens, they must choose between surrendering into docile calcification or taking up heretical arms.
- **The Rebellion's Core:** Every member of **The Severed Root** belongs to this cohort. They fight knowing that even if they avoid an inquisitor's blade, their own bodies will eventually betray them.

## 4. Linguistic Lexicon & World Terms

The following idioms and specialized terms permeate dialogue, quest logs, and item descriptions:

* **The Shroud:** The permanent ash-choked violet sky that extinguished the sun.
* **The Low Sleep:** The dormant subterranean titan whose dreams shape reality and bleed mutagenic black marrow.
* **The Red Tithing:** The periodic church festival where hundreds of young citizens are led in iron chains into the Cathedral Crypts to be sacrificed, soothing the dreaming entity's nightmares and staving off catastrophic waking earthquakes.
* **Warded Tallow:** Rendered animal and human fat treated with herbs and brimstone; its yellowish-green flame is the only light source that repels subterranean mist-aberrations.
* **Marrow-Debt:** The societal and financial debt owed by commoners to the Tallow Guild and Clerisy for salt rations, tallow lanterns, and burial dispensations.
* **The Unsealed:** Slang for adolescents and young adults whose vascular and nervous systems have not yet calcified.
* **Bone-Mortar:** High-grade masonry paste made of pulverized, ossified skeletons used to reinforce the immense bastions of Upper Cinderreach.
* **The Sluice:** The deep subterranean runoff and sewage canal system underneath the city, serving as the hidden arteries of the insurrection.
* **Cold Steel:** The doctrine of relying solely on uncorrupted physical weapons, biomechanical levers, and rigorous military drilling.
* **The Inoculation:** The forbidden surgery of grafting aberration marrow, glands, or ichor into living human flesh to grant aberrant combat capabilities.
* **Calcification:** The progressive, irreversible petrification of soft tissue into brittle, crystalline black bone caused by heretical graft overuse or prolonged eldritch strain.
