## 1. Hub Companions & Key NPC Dossiers

The Sluice Undercroft safehouse is anchored by three primary supporting characters who embody the factional tensions of the setting and provide tier-three mastery progression through distinct personal questlines.

---

### 1.1 Sister Aline Vance (The Apothecary)
* **Age:** 26  
* **Role in Safehouse:** Chief Surgeon, Graft Specialist, and Triage Alchemist at **The Apothecary's Slab**.
* **Visual Appearance:** Tall, gaunt frame; sharp, hawkish features framed by cropped soot-black hair. Wears a defaced, stained clerical habit under a stiffened leather embalmer's apron stained with black vitriol. Her fingers are perpetually stained yellow with sulfur; a leather bandolier of scalpel blades and silver cannulas hangs across her chest.
* **Backstory & Trauma:** Formerly an anointed prosector in the Clerisy's prestigious *Hall of Holy Dissection*. She fled to the underground after discovering that the standard communion draughts distributed to commoners were intentionally spiked with powdered bone sediment to accelerate ossification and induce docile complacency before age 30.
* **Ideology & Voice:** A cold, unsentimental pragmatist. She harbors no illusions of defeating the Low Sleep:
  > *"The god beneath our feet will wake, child. Whether it stirs tomorrow or in a thousand years, steel cannot pierce the mantle of a continent. Our only salvation is to adapt—to graft its flesh into our sinew until we can breathe in the dark."*

#### Personal Quest Arc: *"Anatomy of a Living Saint"*
* **Narrative Premise:** Sister Aline learns that the Clerisy has embalmed the body of *Reliquary Prelate Malakor*, an ancient bishop whose glandular system was infused with concentrated prime-marrow during the first Red Tithing.
* **Mission Objective:** Infiltrate the sealed subterranean crypts of St. Lyra's Cathedral, eliminate the cathedral embalmers, and surgically excise the Prelate's intact pituitary gland-cluster before a **3-Tick Decay Timer** expires.
* **Tier III Master Unlocks:**
  - **Apex Grafts:** Enables grafting of Tier III aberrant organs into player operatives.
  - **Calcification Cleansing:** Surgical procedure at the slab that reverses 1 point of permanent Calcification at the severe cost of **-10% Permanent Max HP**.
  - **Tincture of Still Blood:** Crafts consumable draughts that freeze an operative's Strain Gauge for 1 full combat round.

### 1.2 Bram "Iron-Lung" Karr (The Tinker)
* **Age:** 33 (A rare survivor of the Spent Generation who resisted mental torpor)
* **Role in Safehouse:** Master Armorer, Weaponsmith, and Demolition Expert at **The Tinker's Hearth**.
* **Visual Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, barrel-chested blacksmith with a scarred throat and a rasping, metallic cough caused by inhaling forge soot and bone dust. His right eye is covered by an iron jeweler's loupe; his apron is scorched cowhide studded with iron rivet pouches.
* **Backstory & Trauma:** Formerly the master foundry-man for the *Fourth Kiln Syndicate*. He threw himself into the rebellion after his fourteen-year-old apprentice, Lucan, was arbitrarily seized by the Inquisition as raw biological "flesh-stock" to harvest lung grafts for an injured Prelate.
* **Ideology & Voice:** A fierce, uncompromising Cold Steel purist who despises the Inoculated path:
  > *"Every time you stick that black filth into your arm, you give them a piece of your soul. Put your faith in three inches of honest tempered steel and a balanced pommel. Iron doesn't mutate. Iron doesn't betray you when the bells toll."*

#### Personal Quest Arc: *"Cold Slag, Black Iron"*
* **Narrative Premise:** Bram uncovers that his former supervisor, Foundry-Master Vane, has received a shipment of rare *Star-Slag* ore to forge inquisitorial armor plates.
* **Mission Objective:** Infiltrate *The Crucible Kiln*, manipulate furnace draft levers to flood the lower foundry floor with molten iron hazards, and slay Foundry-Master Vane in single combat before reinforcements arrive.
* **Tier III Master Unlocks:**
  - **Masterwork Steel Forging:** Crafts high-grade physical weaponry (Sunder-Pikes, Spiked Pavises, Flanged Maces) with innate Stagger and armor-stripping perks.
  - **Demolition Satchels:** Equips operatives with throwable shaped charges capable of collapsing stone walls and destroying floor tiles to create chokepoints.
  - **Star-Slag Re-Tempering:** Enhances armor with Star-Slag alloy, granting **+3 Base Armor Rating** and complete immunity to acid/corrosive debuffs.

### 1.3 Tessia "Tess" Morn (The Courier)
* **Age:** 17  
* **Role in Safehouse:** Scout Liaison, Sewer Pathfinder, and Reconnaissance Runner at **The Water-Lock**.
* **Visual Appearance:** Wiry, compact build; dressed in patched leather shingle-armor, grease-smeared wool wraps, and soft calfskin boots. Her left ear is deafened and scarred from a close-range flash-bomb explosion. She carries a notched short-bow and an iron street-crowbar tucked into her sash.
* **Backstory & Trauma:** A lifelong gutter-child of the Ash-Crawl Tenements. Her twelve-year-old younger brother, Pip, was captured during a recent street curfew sweep by the Castellan Wardens and is currently locked in the Seventh Ward holding pen awaiting the next Red Tithing.
* **Ideology & Voice:** A cynical, desperate survivalist who rejects grand revolutionary ideals:
  > *"I don't give a damn about your grand crusade or your dead gods. I need four killers who know how to crack inquisitor skulls, and I need them before the next bell. Help me break Pip out of that pen, or find yourselves another sewer guide."*

#### Personal Quest Arc: *"The Night of the Red Pen"*
* **Narrative Premise:** Tess confirms that Pip has been loaded into Cage Wagon #3 at the Seventh Ward Toll-Pen, scheduled for transfer to the Cathedral Crypts at midnight.
* **Mission Objective:** Infiltrate the heavily guarded Toll-Pen, avoid rotating searchlight beams, breach Cage Wagon #3 with Bram's crowbar, and escort Pip through the drainage canal within a **4-Round Extraction Window** before fortress sirens sound.
* **Tier III Master Unlocks:**
  - **Omniscient Reconnaissance:** Reveals 100% accurate enemy speed tiers, impact ticks, and planned movement trajectories during combat planning phases.
  - **Subterranean Insertion:** Allows the player to deploy up to 2 squad units directly into map flanks or behind enemy lines via sewer grates.
  - **Pip's Runner Network:** Establishes underground trade lines, unlocking black-market access to contraband salt, warded oil, and rare ammunition.
