## 1. Character Taxonomy & Cast Overview

The world of *Emberwake: The Low Sleep* extends far beyond the safehouse walls. Cinderreach is a living, decaying feudal society torn between survival, religious fanaticism, and industrial predation.

NPCs are categorized into three operational spheres:
1. **The Hub Companions (The Sluice Undercroft):** Permanent safehouse anchors who manage workshops and unlock tier-three mastery systems.
2. **Factional Figures & Antagonists (Hostile / Corrupt Authority):** Leaders of the Sovereign Clerisy, Iron Inquisition, and Tallow Guild who shape the overarching conflict.
3. **Neutral & Unaligned Street Cast (The Sunk Ward):** Commoners, black-market fences, conflicted guards, and heretical scholars who provide reconnaissance, trade, moral dilemmas, and branching side quests.

## 2. The Hub Companions (The Sluice Undercroft)

---

### 2.1 Sister Aline Vance (The Apothecary)
* **Age:** 26 | **Faction Origin:** Former Anointed Prosector, Sovereign Clerisy.
* **Role in Safehouse:** Chief Surgeon, Graft Specialist, and Triage Alchemist at **The Apothecary's Slab**.
* **Visual Appearance:** Tall, gaunt frame with cropped soot-black hair. Wears a defaced, stained clerical habit beneath a stiffened leather embalmer's apron stained with black vitriol. Fingers are perpetually yellowed with sulfur; a bandolier of silver scalpels and bone-cannulas hangs across her chest.
* **Backstory & Trauma:** Fled the Clerisy after discovering that the holy communion draughts distributed to commoners were intentionally laced with crushed calcified bone sediment to accelerate ossification and induce docile complacency before age 30.
* **Ideology:** Cold, unsentimental pragmatist. Believes human survival requires embracing cosmic marrow adaptation:
  > *"Steel cannot pierce the mantle of a continent. Our only salvation is to adapt—to graft the titan's flesh into our sinew until we can breathe in the dark."*
* **Personal Quest Arc: *"Anatomy of a Living Saint"***
  - *Objective:* Infiltrate the sealed subterranean crypts of St. Lyra's Cathedral to surgically excise the intact pituitary gland-cluster of an embalmed prelate before an in-mission decay timer expires.
  - *Master Unlocks:* Tier III Apex Grafts, Calcification scraping surgeries (-10% Max HP cost), and *Tincture of Still Blood* (freezes Strain for 1 round).

---

### 2.2 Bram "Iron-Lung" Karr (The Tinker)
* **Age:** 33 (A rare survivor of the Spent Generation who resisted mental torpor) | **Faction Origin:** Master Foundryman, Fourth Kiln.
* **Role in Safehouse:** Master Armorer, Weaponsmith, and Demolition Expert at **The Tinker's Hearth**.
* **Visual Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, barrel-chested blacksmith with a scarred throat and a raspy, metallic cough. Right eye is covered by an iron jeweler's loupe; cowhide apron is scorched and studded with iron rivet pouches.
* **Backstory & Trauma:** Threw himself into the rebellion after his fourteen-year-old apprentice, Lucan, was seized by inquisitors as raw biological "flesh-stock" to harvest lung grafts for an injured prelate.
* **Ideology:** Uncompromising Cold Steel purist who despises the Bitter Inoculation:
  > *"Every time you stick that black filth into your arm, you give them a piece of your soul. Put your faith in three inches of honest tempered steel. Iron doesn't mutate. Iron doesn't betray you when the bells toll."*
* **Personal Quest Arc: *"Cold Slag, Black Iron"***
  - *Objective:* Infiltrate the Crucible Kiln, flood the lower casting floor with molten iron hazards, and slay Foundry-Master Vane in single combat before reinforcements arrive.
  - *Master Unlocks:* Masterwork steel forging (Sunder-Pikes, Spiked Pavises), demolition satchels (collapsing stone walls to create chokepoints), and *Star-Slag Re-Tempering* (+3 Base Armor, acid immunity).

---

### 2.3 Tessia "Tess" Morn & Pip (The Courier Network)
* **Age:** 17 (Tess), 12 (Pip) | **Faction Origin:** Ash-Crawl Tenement Gutter-Runners.
* **Role in Safehouse:** Scout Liaison, Sewer Pathfinder, and Reconnaissance Runner at **The Water-Lock**.
* **Visual Appearance:** Wiry, compact build; dressed in patched leather shingle-armor, grease-smeared wool wraps, and soft calfskin boots. Left ear is scarred and deafened from an inquisitorial flash-bomb.
* **Backstory & Trauma:** Lifelong orphan courier. Her younger brother Pip was captured during a street curfew sweep and locked in a Seventh Ward toll-pen awaiting the Red Tithing.
* **Personal Quest Arc: *"The Night of the Red Pen"***
  - *Objective:* Infiltrate the Seventh Ward Toll-Pen, avoid rotating searchlight cones, breach Cage Wagon #3 with Bram's crowbar, and escort Pip through the drainage canal within a 4-round window.
  - *Master Unlocks:*
    - **Omniscient Reconnaissance:** Reveals 100% accurate enemy speed tiers, impact ticks, and planned movement trajectories during combat planning.
    - **Subterranean Insertion:** Allows deploying up to 2 squad units directly into map flanks via sewer grates.
    - **Pip's Runner Network:** Establishes underground trade lines, unlocking black-market access to contraband salt, warded oil, and rare ammunition.

## 3. The Regime: Sovereign Clerisy & City Watch

---

### 3.1 Arch-Prelate Othemar (The Voice of the Sleep)
* **Faction:** High Sovereign Clerisy (Supreme Theocratic Authority).
* **Role:** The Arch-Antagonist of Cinderreach; overseer of the Red Tithing.
* **Age:** 64 (Ancient; surviving only through daily bone-sedative dialysis and warded tallow chambers).
* **Visual Brief:** Shrunken, skeletal figure draped in heavy cloth-of-gold liturgical copes stiffened with pearl-crusted ossuary lace. Sits upon a wheeled iron sedan chair pushed by deaf-mute acolytes. Half his skull has calcified into smooth black obsidian.
* **Personality & Philosophy:** Not a cartoon tyrant, but a terrifying, tragic theological bureaucrat. He genuinely believes that the continent rests on the slumbering leviathan's brow, and that sacrificing 1,000 youths each cycle is an agonizing mathematical necessity to prevent 500,000 citizens from being swallowed whole:
  > *"You call me a butcher because you see the blood on my altar. But you do not see the beast beneath the stone, child. If I do not feed it the sweet warmth of your youth, it will wake—and its waking will snap this continent like a dry bone."*
* **Narrative Function:** The final ideological barrier of the campaign. The player can defeat him through combat, assassinate him, or expose his private underground titan-communion chambers to cause a church schism.

---

### 3.2 Bailiff Corvo (The Weary Sentinel)
* **Faction:** Sovereign Clerisy / Lower Watch (Third Ward Gatekeeper).
* **Role:** Conflicted Neutral Authority / Corrupt Gatekeeper.
* **Age:** 29 (Stage II Calcification; his left leg is rigid black bone).
* **Visual Brief:** Weather-beaten watchman wearing dented plate over a frayed watchman's tunic. Chews raw brimstone root to dull the persistent pain in his ossified leg.
* **Backstory:** Commands the fortified barricade connecting the Ash-Crawl Tenements to the Fourth Kiln. His 16-year-old sister, Lyra, is showing early signs of heretical marrow sensitivity; he knows she will be selected for the next Tithing unless he can smuggle her out of the city.
* **Gameplay & Narrative Interaction:**
  * *Bribe with Sanctified Salt:* The player can bribe Corvo with Rock Salt or clean medicines to pass district gates without fighting.
  * *Contraband Intel:* Corvo provides accurate patrol schedules that temporarily lower the **Inquisition Alert Meter by -20%**.
  * *The Breaking Point:* If the player conducts a noisy, high-casualty raid in the Third Ward that results in civilian tenement fires, Corvo turns permanently hostile, fortifying his gate with heavy ballistas.

## 4. The Hunters: The Iron Inquisition

---

### 4.1 Inquisitor-General Vaelen (The Blind Eye)
* **Faction:** High Command, The Iron Inquisition.
* **Role:** Supreme Military Inquisitor; Commander of the Purge Squads.
* **Visual Brief:** Towering figure in tailored black-lacquered plate. Wears a smooth, featureless iron face-mask with zero eyeholes, held to his skull by four brass tension-screws. In place of sight, he senses the biological vibration of black marrow through a grafted subterranean tremor-sensor in his collarbone.
* **Ideology & Hypocrisy:** Ruthless enforcer of "Human Purity" who publicly burns anyone showing marrow mutations. However, his private body is heavily augmented with illegal Grade-IV Inoculations, granting him blinding speed and true eldritch perception.
* **Narrative Dynamic:** The relentless pursuer who commands district lockdown sweeps. Every time the **Inquisition Alert Meter** hits 70%, Vaelen releases his personal hunter pack of Render-Beasts to prowl the overworld map.

---

### 4.2 Brother Kenneth (The Broken Penitent)
* **Faction:** The Iron Inquisition (Penitent Caste).
* **Role:** Wandering Neutral / Tragic Informant.
* **Visual Brief:** Barefoot youth draped in shredded burlap; his eyes are clouded from chemical burns, and an iron penitent's collar with inward-facing wire barbs is riveted around his neck.
* **Backstory:** Formerly an inquisitorial acolyte who tried to help his younger sister escape a culling transport. Discovered and punished with sensory mutilation, he wanders the foggy alleys singing cathedral hymns to warn unsealed youths before the purge squads arrive.
* **Gameplay Interaction:**
  * Encountered as a wandering neutral event on Sunk Ward street nodes.
  * *Dialogue Choice (Mercy vs. Intel):* Giving him a flask of clean water or clean cloth causes him to recite coded liturgical ciphers that reveal hidden drainage shortcuts and bypass searchlight cones on the overworld map.

## 5. The Underworld: The Tallow Guild & The Outlaws

---

### 5.1 Madame Vespera (The Grease Queen)
* **Faction:** The Tallow Guild (Underground Smuggler / Independent Broker).
* **Role:** Proprietor of *"The Leaking Vat"* (Subterranean Speakeasy & Black Market).
* **Visual Brief:** Voluptuous, middle-aged woman wearing a bodice fashioned from dyed leather and grease-resistant oiled silks, adorned with rings carved from yellow tallow-amber. Smokes a long clay pipe filled with dried sulfur herbs.
* **Personality & Philosophy:** Pure capitalist survivalist. Despises the church for taxing tallow, but distrusts the Severed Root for their reckless ideological martyrdom:
  > *"Rebellion is an expensive hobby, young blood. The church wants your bones; the rebels want your blood. Me? I only want your coin. Gold doesn't care if you turn to stone tomorrow."*
* **Hub & Gameplay Interaction:**
  * *Black Market Stash:* Sells rare contraband that Bram cannot forge (foreign arbalest parts, stolen communion sedatives, warded lantern oil).
  * *Intel Brokerage:* Buys stolen Clerisy ledgers and officer insignia, converting them into safehouse Scrap Iron and Gold.

---

### 5.2 "Nails" Morik (The Bone-Shaper)
* **Faction:** Independent Tenement Outlaw / Craftsman.
* **Role:** Neutral Artisan / Black-Market Bone Carver.
* **Visual Brief:** Short, hunchbacked artisan with blackened teeth and thick leather apron stuffed with carving gouges and bone-files. Wears a magnifying lens strapped to his forehead.
* **Crafting Specialty:** Specializes in carving tools, counterfeit church seals, and armor-piercing bodkins directly out of calcified human bone.
* **Gameplay Integration:**
  * If supplied with **Calcified Bone Shards** from slain husks, Morik carves unique armor-shredding quarrels for Bran and barbed cestuses for Torin at a fraction of the cost of refined iron.

## 6. The Commoners: The Tenements & The Spent Generation

---

### 6.1 Old Mother Agnes (The Tenement Matriarch)
* **Faction:** The Spent Generation (Ash-Crawl Commoners).
* **Age:** 44 (One of the oldest surviving commoners in Lower Cinderreach).
* **Visual Brief:** Stooped woman whose forearms and lower jaw have completely petrified into brittle, chalkboard-textured black stone. Wears a faded woolen shawl and sits on a rickety wooden stool in the courtyard of Tenement Block 12.
* **Lore Tether:** Retains clear memories of the time before The Extinguishing when celestial stars could be seen in the sky. She runs an illegal orphanage in the tenement attics, concealing unsealed children whose parents have calcified into docile catatonia.
* **Moral Dilemma Side Quest: *"The Rafters of Tenement 12"***
  - An inquisitorial purge squad conducts a surprise chimney sweep on Block 12.
  - The squad can intervene to wipe out the inquisitors, protecting Agnes and the children, but permanently spiking the **Inquisition Alert Meter by +35%**.
  - Alternatively, staying hidden preserves safehouse secrecy, but leads to the children being culled and Agnes being dragged to the bone-mills.

---

### 6.2 Lucan (The Rescued Apprentice)
* **Faction:** Formerly The Fourth Kiln; The Severed Root Youth Ward.
* **Age:** 14 (Unsealed).
* **Visual Brief:** Skinny, soot-stained youth with a burn scar across his cheek resembling a gear tooth. Wears oversized work trousers held up by a frayed rope.
* **Narrative Integration:** Bram Karr's former forge apprentice, thought lost to the dissection slabs.
* **Rescue Mission (Phase 2):**
  - Liberating Lucan from a culling wagon during a Phase 2 raid provides a major emotional turning point for Bram Karr.
  - *Permanent Benefit:* Lucan joins Bram at the Tinker's Hearth, increasing crafting speed, lowering Scrap Iron upgrade costs by **20%**, and unlocking Bram's unique masterwork blueprints.

## 7. The Heretical Fringe: Watchers of the Low Sleep

---

### 7.1 Brother Malachi (The Hermit of the Fissure)
* **Faction:** The Heretical Apostates / Titan Cult.
* **Role:** Cosmic Scholar / Eldritch Lorekeeper.
* **Visual Brief:** Wild-eyed recluse living in the flooded basalt catacombs beneath the storm-drains. His hair is matted with purple marrow slime; crystalline black bone spikes sprout organically along his spine. He wears robes woven from dried sewer reed and warded tallow wax.
* **Psychological Condition:** His mind has partially synchronized with the subconscious dream-waves of the Low Sleep titan. Speaks in disturbing, poetic prophecies that alternate between gibberish and chillingly accurate tactical insights.
* **Gameplay Integration:**
  * *Tremor Forecasting:* Reveals upcoming seismic tremor hazard tiles on the tactical map before combat starts.
  * *Aberration Lore:* Decodes harvested *Apex Marrow Glands*, unlocking their true hidden passive traits before Sister Aline performs surgery.
  * *Cosmic Warning:* Warns the player of the exact day and hour the Cathedral Bells will toll, allowing the squad to plan expeditions between resonance waves.
