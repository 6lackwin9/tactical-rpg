## 1. Subterranean Safehouse: The Sluice Undercroft

The operational headquarters of The Severed Root sits three levels beneath the basalt pavement of Lower Cinderreach. Once a municipal storm-drain and Roman-style sewage runoff built centuries prior to The Extinguishing, it has been converted into a fortified, clandestine redoubt.

### 1.1 Safehouse Installations & Functional Wings

1. **The Apothecary's Slab (Sister Aline Vance):**
   - *Physical Space:* A massive, blood-grooved granite embalming slab surrounded by glass retorts, salt canisters, iron bone-saws, and amber preservative jars.
   - *Function:* Operatives undergo traumatic graft installations, receive sulfur-chemical flushes to vent dormant strain floors, and undergo high-tier Calcification scraping surgeries.
2. **The Tinker's Hearth (Bram "Iron-Lung" Karr):**
   - *Physical Space:* An improvised, ventless charcoal forge built against an ancient flanged brick flue. Water-baffles prevent telltale sparks or smoke plumes from rising into city vents.
   - *Function:* Banging out dented iron breastplates, re-tempering cold steel weapons, affixing lead counter-weights to polearms (+Stagger tuning), and assembling smoke/ash bombs.
3. **The Water-Lock & Dead-Drop Basin:**
   - *Physical Space:* A half-submerged iron portcullis where stagnant runoff is filtered through charcoal baffles.
   - *Function:* Serves as the cell's gear stash. Submerged courier tubes receive coded bone-ciphers and patrol logs dropped through surface gutters by street sympathizers.
4. **The Dry Hollows (Rest & Quarantine):**
   - *Physical Space:* A sequence of recessed, dry brick kiln vaults lined with coarse straw and moth-eaten wool blankets.
   - *Function:* Where exhausted recruits sleep off combat fatigue, nurse infected puncture wounds, or sit in darkness through high-Strain psychological quarantine until their aberrant psychosis subsides.

## 2. Overworld Campaign Map: The Sunk Ward

The primary operational theatre for Act I is **The Sunk Ward**—the lowest, most squalid industrial quarter of Cinderreach, nestled against the foot of the city's gargantuan basalt curtain walls.

### 2.1 Key Regional Locations

* **The Drowned Drainage Grate:** The concealed egress point beneath a collapsed canal arch. Rebels wade through knee-deep sludge under cover of bell-chimes to emerge into the city's alleyways.
* **Ash-Crawl Tenements:** Sprawling multi-story slums built from rotting timber and recycled bone-brick. Home to thousands of spent laborers. Filled with loose floorboards, blind alleys, and desperate informants who will either shelter a rebel or sell them out for salt.
* **The Tallow Vats & Bone-Mills:** Gargantuan iron caldrons bubbling with rendered animal fat and unclaimed corpses. Guarded by mercenary guild-enforcers and bone-stokers. A primary target for sabotage and fuel raids.
* **The Crucible Watchpost:** A heavily fortified stone bastion spanning the narrow gorge between Lower and Upper Cinderreach. Manning point for inquisitorial crossbowmen, searchlight towers, and heavy armored castellans.
* **The Slag Gate:** The massive iron portcullis sealing the mountain pass leading out toward the Upper Bastion and the highland ash wastes.

## 3. Environmental Hazards & Map Conditions

Combat encounters in The Sunk Ward feature unique environmental hazards that demand constant repositioning:

* **Tar-Bogs (Liquefied Earth):** Seismic tremors from the Low Sleep cause cobblestone sections to liquefy into boiling black pitch. Units stepping onto a tar tile suffer -2 Movement and become susceptible to being ignited.
* **Inquisitorial Searchlights:** Tower-mounted mirrors illuminated by magnesium flame. Stepping into a lit tile immediately reveals stealthed units, alerts all enemies on the map, and boosts enemy hit chance by +20%.
* **Warded Tallow Smoke vs. Blight Mist:** Normal mist tiles obscure vision and inflict +5% Strain per turn; smoke zones created by burning warded tallow disperse the blight and grant cover without increasing corruption.
* **Molten Slag Runoff:** Periodic foundry runoff vents across foundry maps on specific timeline ticks (e.g., Tick 5), incinerating anyone trapped on gutter channels.
