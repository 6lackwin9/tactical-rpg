## 1. Macro-Geography: The Stratified Tiers of Cinderreach

The city of Cinderreach is built vertically into the jagged caldera of an extinct volcano whose sub-basalt depths cradle the slumbering cosmic titan of **The Low Sleep**. The city is divided into three distinct vertical tiers, each defining one major campaign act:

```text
========================================================================================
                          THE VERTICAL STRATA OF CINDERREACH
========================================================================================

    [ TIER 2: UPPER CINDERREACH / THE HIGH BASTION ]  <-- ACT 2: THE FIRST RUPTURE
    ├── St. Lyra's Cathedral & Grand Belltower
    ├── The Gilded Avenues & Clerisy Sanctuaries
    ├── Tallow Guild Reservoirs & Inquisitorial Penitentiary
    └── Forward Outpost: The Scribe's Crypt (8 Bunks)
                                │
                        [ THE SLAG GATE ] (Crucible Climax / Phase Transition)
                                │
    [ TIER 1: LOWER CINDERREACH / THE SUNK WARD ]     <-- ACT 1: THE YOUNG VANGUARD
    ├── Ash-Crawl Slums & Tenement Warrens
    ├── The Fourth Kiln & Bone-Crushing Foundries
    ├── The Corpse-Rendering Tallow Vats & Sluice Canals
    └── Primary Hub: The Sluice Undercroft (6 Bunks)
                                │
                      [ THE BASALT CHASM RIFT ] (Cathedral Crypt Descent)
                                │
    [ TIER 3: THE SUB-BASALT ABYSS / MANTLE FISSURE ] <-- ACT 3: THE AWAKENING ABYSS
    ├── The Weeping Basalt Chasm & Petrified Arches
    ├── Living Marrow Conduits & Awakening Chorus Spire
    ├── High Altar of the First Sleep & The Dreaming Titan Core
    └── Expedition FOB: The Weeping Redoubt
========================================================================================
```

## 2. Safehouse Hub & Forward Outpost Progression

As The Severed Root ascends through the city and descends into the abyss, their base of operations evolves across three operational footholds:

```text
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| CAMPAIGN PHASE        | OPERATIONAL BASE            | WING EXPANSIONS & UPGRADES    | RECOVERY & CAPACITY              |
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Act 1 (Phase 1)       | The Sluice Undercroft       | Bram's Hearth, Sister Aline's | 6 Bunks (Original Founders only).|
|                       | (Lower Storm-Drain Vaults)  | Slab, Water-Lock Stash.       | Cold steel repairs & basic salves|
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Act 2 (Phase 2)       | Forward Relay: The Scribe's | Dissection Rig, High-grade    | 8 Bunks (Recruits + Survivors).  |
|                       | Crypt (St. Lyra's Basement) | Grafting, Courier Dead-Drops. | Inoculation surgeries & flushes. |
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Act 3 (Phase 3)       | Expedition FOB: The Weeping | Portable Quench-Anvil, Ward-  | 8 Bunks (Hardened Veterans).     |
|                       | Redoubt (Basalt Cavern)     | Lantern Rigs, Sanity Baffles. | High Strain isolation & surgery. |
+-----------------------+-----------------------------+-------------------------------+----------------------------------+
```

### 2.1 Hub 1: The Sluice Undercroft (Act 1 Primary Redoubt)
Located three levels beneath the basalt cobbles of Lower Cinderreach in an ancient municipal storm-drain:
1. **The Apothecary's Slab (Sister Aline Vance):** Granite embalming slab for trauma surgery, salt flushes, and Stage I Calcification stabilization.
2. **The Tinker's Hearth (Bram "Iron-Lung" Karr):** Ventless charcoal forge with water-baffles for Cold Steel weapon re-tempering, shield reinforcement, and Stagger lead-weighting.
3. **The Water-Lock & Dead-Drop Basin (Madame Tessia):** Submerged portcullis with charcoal baffles for secret bone-cipher drops and supply caching.
4. **The Dry Hollows:** 6 brick kiln resting bunks for fatigue recovery, psychological decompression, and quarantine.

### 2.2 Hub 2: The Scribe's Crypt (Act 2 Forward Relay)
Discovered in the flooded catacombs beneath St. Lyra’s Cathedral. Replaces the Sluice as the primary tactical launchpad for Upper Bastion operations:
* **Expanded Capacity (8 Bunks):** Accommodates surviving founders and newly liberated procedural recruits rescued from culling transports.
* **Sister Aline's Surgical Rig:** Full Apex Grafting operating theater, allowing cranial, thoracic, and visceral organ grafts.
* **The Scribe's Courier Blind:** Intercepts church liturgical dispatches, displaying moving Inquisitorial patrol routes and culling wagon locations.

### 2.3 Hub 3: The Weeping Redoubt (Act 3 Expedition FOB)
A fortified cavern outpost hammered into the petrified marrow arches flanking the Mantle Fissure:
* **The Portable Quench-Anvil:** Bram Karr's high-heat expedition forge, burning concentrated titan ichor to forge Black-Iron penetrators and Stagger counterweights.
* **Ward-Lantern Rig:** A centralized beacon burning warded tallow that prevents the creeping atmospheric Strain of the Abyss from maddening resting operatives.
* **Isolation Vaults:** Reinforced iron cages where Stage IV Calcified operatives are safely restrained during bell tremors to prevent psychotic outbursts.

## 3. The Three Operational Theatres

### 3.1 Act 1: The Sunk Ward (Lower Cinderreach)
* **Theme & Tone:** Industrial grimdark, drowning grime, squalor, desperate survival.
* **Key Landmarks:** The Drowned Drainage Grate, Ash-Crawl Tenements, Fourth Kiln Foundry, The Tallow Vats, Crucible Watchpost, Slag Gate.
* **Detailed Blueprint:** [sunk_ward_campaign_map.md](file:///c:/dev/tactical-rpg/games/emberwake/lore/background/sunk_ward_campaign_map.md).

### 3.2 Act 2: The High Bastion & St. Lyra's Cathedral District (Upper Cinderreach)
* **Theme & Tone:** Gothic monumentalism, oppressive wealth, magnesium glare, clerical terror, transhuman corruption behind gilded façades.
* **Key Landmarks:** The Slag Gate Breach, Gilded Boulevards, Merchant Tallow Reservoirs, St. Lyra's Embalming Cloister, Culling Stockades, Grand Belltower, Reliquary Crypts, The Grand Spire Gate.
* **Detailed Blueprint:** [upper_bastion_campaign_map.md](file:///c:/dev/tactical-rpg/games/emberwake/lore/background/upper_bastion_campaign_map.md).

### 3.3 Act 3: The Mantle Fissure & Titan Catacombs (Sub-Basalt Abyss)
* **Theme & Tone:** Cosmic dread, petrified anatomy, pulsing eldritch marrow, deafening acoustic vibrations, ticking apocalypse.
* **Key Landmarks:** The Chasm Ingress, Weeping Basalt Chasm, Pulsing Marrow Conduits, The Awakening Chorus Redoubt, The Silent Crypt of the Apostles, High Altar of the First Sleep, The Dreaming Titan Core.
* **Detailed Blueprint:** [mantle_fissure_campaign_map.md](file:///c:/dev/tactical-rpg/games/emberwake/lore/background/mantle_fissure_campaign_map.md).

## 4. Comprehensive Environmental Hazard Taxonomy Across All Acts

Each tier of Cinderreach possesses distinctive tactical map hazards that alter positioning, movement, and resource management:

```text
+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| ACT / TIER            | ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARD      | TACTICAL RULESET & ENGINE IMPACT                                |
+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| Act 1: Sunk Ward      | Tar-Bogs (Liquefied Earth)| -2 Movement penalty; inflicts 'Flammable' status.               |
|                       | Inquisitorial Searchlights| Reveals stealth; alerts all hostiles; grants enemies +20% Acc.  |
|                       | Warded Smoke vs Blight Fog| Fog: +5% Strain/turn; Warded Smoke: grants cover, blocks strain.|
|                       | Molten Slag Runoffs       | Bursts on set timeline ticks (e.g. T5); 25 direct fire damage.  |
+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| Act 2: High Bastion   | Magnesium Spotlights      | High-intensity searchlights; triggers immediate Alert spike.    |
|                       | Resonating Curfew Chimes  | At T1, all combatants roll Composure; failure = -10 Poise.      |
|                       | Blessed Phosphor Sprays   | Cathedral ground trap; ignites oil and deals 8 true damage/tick.|
|                       | Crumbling Parapet Gaps    | Rooftop edge tiles; knockback causes instant lethal fall.       |
+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| Act 3: Mantle Fissure | Liquefied Basalt Flues    | Boiling stone; moving onto tile inflicts 20 heat + melts armor. |
|                       | Seismic Fracture Tremors  | Battlefield shake every 3 rounds; shifts hazard tiles by 1 cell.|
|                       | Pulsing Marrow Conduits   | Stepping onto vein grants +100% Inoculated dmg but +15% Strain. |
|                       | Atmospheric Strain Mists  | All operatives passively gain +3% Strain each round (Unwarded). |
+-----------------------+---------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
```
