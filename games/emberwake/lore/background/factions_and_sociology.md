## 1. The Socio-Political Hierarchy

Society in Emberwake is an ecclesiastical police state built on scarcity, industrial rendering of the dead, and terror of the subterranean entity. Power flows directly downward from the cathedrals through paramilitary inquisitors and monopolistic guilds down to the subjugated laborers.

```text
               [ THE SOVEREIGN CLERISY ]  <=== High Hegemony (Blood Pacts & Cathedral Wards)
                       /          \
                      /            \
        [ THE TALLOW GUILD ]   [ THE IRON INQUISITION ]

         & Bone Mortar)          Culling & Curfews)
                      \            /

                         |
```

## 2. Faction Dossiers

### 2.1 The Sovereign Clerisy (The Ruling Elite)
* **Societal Function:** High ecclesiastical aristocracy acting as the divine wardens and ritual caretakers of the dreaming entity beneath the earth.
* **Theological Doctrine:** 
  - Proclaims that physical suffering is sanctified and that unquestioning obedience to the Church staves off total cosmic annihilation.
  - Sells the belief that humanity's original sin disturbed the leviathan's slumber, and only through perpetual tithes of blood, labor, and silence can the continent be kept from sinking into the black tar-sea.
* **The Red Tithing:** An annual or emergency holy event. Cathedral bell-tolls summon thousands to the plazas; hundreds of adolescent youths are selected by the Hierarchs and herded in chains down into the subterranean catacombs as live pacification offerings to appease the titan's nightmare tremors.
* **Visual Identity:** 
  - Polished, featureless windowless iron masks with narrow slit perforations for breath.
  - Bleached horsehair vestments stiffened with warded beeswax.
  - Heavy swinging golden censers burning toxic, narcotic resins that dull sensation and induce reverent stupor in crowds.

### 2.2 The Iron Inquisition & Castellan Wardens (The Paramilitary Arm)
* **Societal Function:** The enforcement branch of the Clerisy. They patrol the stone ramparts, enforce sector curfews, operate juvenile holding pens, and violently root out unregistered mutants and subversive cells.
* **Combat Doctrine:**
  - Heavily armored foot soldiers clad in overlapping fluted iron plates and kettle helms.
  - Equipped with billhooks, halberds, notched sunder-pikes, and spiked kite shields.
  - Specialize in **Stance-Breaking and Stagger Attacks**, systematically exhausting opponents' posture before bludgeoning them into submission.
  - Utilize blinding magnesium flash-bombs to disorient suspects and disable ambushers in narrow city alleys.
* **The Culling Pens:** Walled stockades located at every ward gate where young citizens are detained without trial for biometric inspections (checking for subcutaneous bone growths or black blood).

### 2.3 The Tallow Guild & The Bone-Mills (Industrial Monopolies)
* **Societal Function:** The industrial backbone of civilization. They operate the massive, soot-belching rendering vats and crushing mills bordering the city slums.
* **Economic Monopolies:**
  - **Warded Tallow:** Renders animal carcasses, livestock, and unredeemed human dead into warded tallow oil. This fuel is mandatory for domestic lamps; burning standard wood or raw coal attracts predatory shadow-aberrations from the vents.
  - **Bone-Mortar:** Grinds calcified, ossified human remains into ultra-dense structural cement required to repair the crumbling foundations of Upper Cinderreach.
  - **Salt Ledgers:** Controls the imports of sanctified preservation rock-salt, rationing it strictly to enforce compliance among neighborhood wardens.
* **Dynamic:** Despised by commoners as butcher-merchants, yet indispensable. To insult a Guild Tallow-Master is to have one's lantern oil ration revoked, consigning a household to darkness and death.

### 2.4 The Docile Masses (The Subjugated Majority)
* **Societal Function:** The millions of common laborers, mill-hands, seamstresses, and stonemasons aged 25 and older.
* **Psychological State:** Bound by physical exhaustion, mild sedative communion draughts, and lung-rot from tallow smoke. They consider rebellion foolish and suicidal; most parents will bind their own rebellious adolescent children and hand them over to inquisitors to preserve the household's salt and oil rations.

## 3. The Severed Root (The Insurrection)

The Severed Root is the decentralized, desperate resistance movement founded and manned by **The Unsealed** (ages 15–24). Operating out of sunken drainage networks and forgotten pre-cataclysm crypts, they wage a war of survival against impossible odds.

### 3.1 Organizational Structure: The Rule of Three
To withstand the Inquisition's torture and magical scrying, the rebellion enforces strict operational compartmentalization:
- **Autonomous Cells:** Operatives operate strictly in cells of three or four (the exact composition of a tactical combat squad).
- **The Blind Chain:** No operative knows the identity or hideout of more than two external contacts. If a cell is captured, they can compromise only their immediate handler before the chain goes dark.
- **Bone-Ciphers:** Messages are carved onto etched fragments of black bone dropped into submerged grates and retrieved at designated water-lock basins.

### 3.2 The Great Ideological Fracture
Within the rebellion, two irreconcilable philosophies vie for control over the soul of the resistance:

```text
                 [ THE SEVERED ROOT ]
                         /  \
                        /    \
                       /      \
            [ THE PURISTS ]   [ THE INOCULATED ]
            - Cold Steel      - Aberrant Marrow Grafts
            - Human Will      - Transhuman Adaptation
            - Demolition      - Piercing Eldritch Burst
            - Zero Corruption - The Calcification Clock
```

#### The Purists (Cold Steel Loyalists)
* **Philosophy:** Led by veteran smiths and former garrison line-soldiers (such as Bram "Iron-Lung" Karr). They believe that grafting aberration flesh into human bodies is an act of spiritual surrender that turns rebels into the very monsters they fight.
* **Methods:** Rely exclusively on uncorrupted physical discipline, biomechanical leverage, counter-weights, armor plating, precision ambushes, and sapping demolition charges.
* **Advantage:** Units suffer 0% Strain and zero permanent calcification. Their combat longevity across a prolonged campaign is indefinite.

#### The Inoculated (Resonant Flesh-crafters)
* **Philosophy:** Championed by radical heretic apothecaries and desperate frontline fighters (such as Sister Aline Vance). They argue that confronting cosmic horrors and armored inquisitorial shock-troops with mundane iron swords is romantic suicide.
* **Methods:** Harvest glands, marrow, nerve-clusters, and ichor from slain aberrations and inquisitorial officers. Through crude, excruciating field surgery, these organs are spliced into living operatives, granting supernatural speed, phase shifting, and acid bile discharge.
* **Disadvantage:** Every heretical maneuver spikes in-combat **Strain**. Ending battles under high strain inexorably ticks the character toward **Stage V Calcification** (irreversible loss/mutation).
