# Design preferences

Owner: Design thread. Firm rules: `docs/decisions/`. Format: preference (date, source).

- Enemy morale is shown as flavour states (Steady, Shaken, Wavering, Broken), never as numbers (2026-10-18, MORALE1 approval).
- Empowered is a story reward, never a boss-fight mechanic; no party buff at a boss phase change (2026-10-18, PACE1).
- Option branches are paused: Design proposes ideas and Tushar reviews and approves them (2026-10-18 08:29).
- One world first: per-world work waits until the Ashlands are complete (2026-10-18).
- Fights should not drag: mobs about 3 rounds, elites about 5, bosses about 8 in two acts (a mechanic-check phase 1, a short lethal phase 2) (2026-10-18 09:24, PACE1).
- Bosses, elites and reckless units ignore morale; ending a fight by routing the stragglers is welcome, and enemies who flee count against their group (2026-10-18 09:02-09:20, MORALE1).
- Hide engine detail from the player (AI behaviour text, multipliers, coordinates) to cut information bloat (2026-10-18 10:05, combat UI feedback).
- Light-touch battle tuning: the fewest data changes that hit the bands (2026-10-18, DR1/DR2).
