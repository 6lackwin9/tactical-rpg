# Decisions: the Low Sleep design (Emberwake: A Scarlet Lullaby)

Settled; do not re-litigate without asking the user. Scope: the new campaign's design (the worlds after the Ashlands)
and the lore library `games/emberwake/docs/lore/`. The other decision files keep governing the Ashlands, which stay the
kit's sample and parity world on the classic ruleset; where a row here differs from them, it applies to the new worlds
only. The detail lives in `games/emberwake/docs/lore/CANON.md`; the brainstorm's own log is
`games/emberwake/docs/lore/specs/worldbuilding_decisions.md` (DEC-LORE-01 onward). Index and global rules:
`docs/DECISIONS.md`; superseded rows: `docs/decisions/archive.md`. Append new rows at the end of the table.
