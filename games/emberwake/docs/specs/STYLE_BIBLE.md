# Emberwake style bible

Status: locked (Tushar, 2026-11-17, style-lock board `art_src/style/board.py`: picks P3, F2, S2, I2). Owner: Art/UI.
Every new or replaced asset is checked against this page and `art_src/style/palette.json` before it is placed. Status of
every asset: [ASSET_STATUS.md](ASSET_STATUS.md) (generated by `art_src/status/asset_status.py`).

## 1. One look

Soot-gothic, low-key, warm light in cold iron. The UI's Tallow and Iron theme (`ui/theme.json`) is the master palette:
void and basalt blacks, slate, iron grey, oxblood, rust, amber light, parchment. Nothing is heraldic-bright: no pure
reds, royal blues or white robes. Light comes from fire and tallow (amber), never from the sky.

## 2. Portraits (P3 colour, F2 frame)

- **Colour (P3, Tallow and Iron grade)**: every portrait gets the same finishing grade after generation: saturation
  x 0.75, then a 28 % blend toward a colourised greyscale (shadows `#120c0c`, mids `#78604e`, lights `#ece0c8`)
  (`palette.json` `portrait_grade`; `art_src/style/board.py` `tallow()`). Applied in the portrait finisher, so a raw
  generation is never placed ungraded.
- **Frame (F2)**: a 2 px iron line `#4b5563` with a 2 px oxblood top line `#991b1b`, on the panel colour. The frame is
  drawn by the UI, not painted into the picture; portraits keep a dark, plain background.
- **Content**: bust, three-quarter view, one hand of the same painter (the founder set is the reference for framing and
  light). Day 1 founders are clean mortals (CANON 3.1, 3.3): no spurs, beast eyes, grafts or veins.
- **Characters come from Flow** (Tushar 2026-11-17): Flow sets up each character, then makes its detailed sprites,
  animations and story CGs (one person across all). Every current portrait, face sheet and sprite sheet is a
  placeholder (ASSET_STATUS). The Gemini web queue keeps making character images as experiments and as the reference
  images that set up each character in the Flow project; one may sit at the final path as a stand-in, graded P3.

## 3. Battle sprites (S2, muted three-tone)

- Final sprites and animations come from Flow (section 2). Until then the procedural rig (`tools/sprites`) stays as
  the placeholder, and the S2 rules below apply to it.
- **Paint**: every palette slot ships a full ramp (base, `_lo`, `_hi`), not only its base colour. Shadow: value x 0.62,
  saturation x 1.1, hue stepped up to 3.5 % toward blue-violet; light: value x 1.22, saturation x 0.85, hue toward amber
  (`palette.json` `sprite_ramp`; `art_src/style/sprites.py` `ramp()`). Today the engine swaps only base colours, so the
  class's bright shades show through; the ramp needs palette files that carry the shades (a Build change, see Outbox).
- **Colours**: muted, saturation at most 0.55, values between 0.18 and 0.80, oxblood not red, iron not steel blue.
  Reference palettes: Kaelen and the Castellan Watch footman in `palette.json` `sprite_reference`.
- **Named characters** get their own head (hair, hat) and outfit parts so the token matches the portrait (Kaelen:
  shoulder-length golden-blond hair, cream gambeson, scarred breastplate). A palette swap alone is not enough.
- Readable on the dark battle floor at 1x: the figure's mean value stays at least 0.15 above the ground's.

## 4. Icons (I2, slate tile with a painted glyph)

- One template for skills, items and statuses: a rounded dark slate tile (fill `#303a46` to `#37474f`, rim `#4c606b`,
  8 px corner radius; skills 64 px, items 96 px octagonal), with **one** painted object on it, at most four main
  colours, readable at 32 px (the battle bar).
- The painted-on-red full tiles (I1) and the flat glyphs (I3) move to this template.
- Prompt for icon sheets: "a single [object], painted pixel art, centred on a flat #FF00FF background, no tile, no
  frame, no text"; the tile is added in code when the icon is cut, so every icon shares the exact same tile.

## 5. Grounds, props and region maps

- **Reference first** (Tushar 2026-11-17): every region map starts with one generated layout reference image of the
  whole map, front-on top-down; the map is then rebuilt with tiles and props to match it and shown beside it.
- Props: straight front-on top-down, no cast oval shadows, a tight dark contact line (ART_UI_STUDIO 3.3).
- Grounds: dark and quiet under small figures, mean brightness 24 to 40, spread at most 14 (`palette.json` `ground`);
  every ground prompt says "dark and low-contrast" in so many words.

## 6. Backdrops, CGs and music

- Backdrops and story cards follow the VN-stage set (the four VN stages, the M5 gate): painted, low-key, amber light.
  The procedural backdrops and story cards are placeholders (ASSET_STATUS).
- **What a Gemini image may be** (Tushar 2026-11-18: "only thing that is probably ok/approved directly from gemini
  webapp are assets that are not locked to the game itself and would go in the kits asset library like tilesets, props,
  skill and gear icons"): generic library art (tilesets, props, skill and gear icons) can be approved straight from the
  Gemini web queue. Anything tied to this game (story CGs, backdrops of story beats, characters, story art) made there
  is a placeholder until its Flow version replaces it at the same path; opening CGs are made on the web queue for now
  ("lets use gemini webapp for now, but note that these will be placeholder").
- **Placeholders in the kit library** (Tushar 2026-11-18): placeholder characters, backgrounds and CGs may also go into
  the kit asset library (`kits/rpg/assets/`) as generic placeholder sets, stripped of every game reference (no names,
  lore or places) and carrying only asset metadata (kind, style, size, palette, tags, status). The library may later
  hold sets in several styles. The metadata format is to be agreed with Design and the tools and engine lane; until
  then nothing is copied there.
- Music: Flow Music (Lyria, house seed `455684`) for finals; synth tracks are placeholders.

## 7. Batch check (every batch, one contact sheet)

Batches of 10 to 30 same-type assets. Each returns one contact sheet at game size and at 4x, before and after, on the
real background, scored on five points: **identity** (matches canon brief and the character's other art), **palette**
(within `palette.json`), **pixel grid** (one pixel size, no blur, no halo), **readable at size**, **canon** (CANON.md
tags). Tushar looks once per batch; rejects go back with one note; replacements land in place at the same path.
