## 1. The story in one page (`[LOCKED]`)

**Cinderreach** sits in a volcanic caldera atop a sleeping titan (**the Low Sleep**). Two centuries ago **the Extinguishing** (`b2`: cause stays a mystery) hid sun and stars behind **the Shroud** (**never broken in any ending**); cathedral bells keep the hours in a **Soot-Gothic + Early-Industrial Ossuary** city (crossbows/arbalests & chemical sprayers; **no gunpowder firearms**). The **Sovereign Clerisy** rules via the **Iron Inquisition**, **Castellan Watch**, and **Tallow Guild & Fourth Kiln Syndicate**. Adults `25+` (**the Spent**) are dulled by bone-sediment communion draughts and tallow smoke; only youths `under 25` (**the Unsealed**) resist (`18-24` static operative ages, `CL-07`; children `under 18` are non-combat stakes only).

The player leads **the Severed Root** from the **Sluice Undercroft**, guided by three sharp-minded Spent mentors: **`Berek "Cinder-Lung" Dorn`** (`33`, Cold Steel), **`Chirurgeon Sabina`** (`31`, retort-refined ossuary bone-pins), and **`Talia Rusk`** (`29`, bone-cipher spymaster sheltering `12`-yo `Ivo`, backed by fence **`Widow Varka`**). All `6` Founders start **100% clean mortal human on Day 1**.

**Central truth (`Option A` + real waking catastrophe, `[LOCKED]`):** **Waking the titan genuinely cracks Cinderreach's caldera and swallows the city**, and **only the dream steeps Unsealed remains into ageless rendered marrow**. Two centuries ago, the **First Extinguishers** refused to kill the titan's brainstem with black iron, staged the **first Great Tithing** of thousands under cover of the Extinguishing, built **Lullaby Houses** (`Kaelen` raised there, `Ysolde` sang there), and pumped `Lazar`'s rendered marrow up Fourth-Kiln brass **Marrow Conduits** (`Berek` cast their valves unknowingly) so the Clerisy (`Othemar` `64`) never ages while dead Spent become **Warded Tallow** and **bone-mortar**.
