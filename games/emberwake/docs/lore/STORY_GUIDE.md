## 1. The story in one page

Cinderreach sits in a volcanic caldera over a sleeping titan (**the Low Sleep**). Two centuries ago **the Extinguishing** hid sky and stars behind **the Shroud**; cathedral bells keep the hours. The **Sovereign Clerisy** rules via the **Iron Inquisition**, **Castellan Watch**, and **Tallow Guild** (rendering the dead into lamp fat). Adults `25+` (**the Spent**) are dulled by bone-sediment draughts and tallow fumes; only youths `under 25` (**the Unsealed**) resist. Publicly, the yearly **Red Tithing** is the lullaby keeping the titan asleep.

The player leads **the Severed Root** (`18-24` Unsealed operatives) from the **Sluice Undercroft**. Six founders join in Act 1; `Bram` (Cold Steel) and `Sister Aline` (grafts) run the hub on a `42`-day Doom Clock.

**Central truth (`Q-01 A`, `[LOCKED]`)**: The lullaby is a harvest. **Lullaby Houses** raise tithe-children from infancy while ward sweeps fill the count. Sacrifices laid over dreaming veins are steeped by the dream; `Lazar`'s Embalming Cloisters render the remains into **rendered marrow** (younger = purer), pumped to St. Lyra's so the Clerisy never ages (`Othemar` is `64` while Spent rarely pass `45`). Dead Spent become **Warded Tallow** ("young -> marrow, old -> fuel"). Waking the titan ends the harvest, not the world. **Tone rule (`[LOCKED]`)**: children are stakes only, never fighters or on-screen victims.

**Precedent (`[LOCKED]`)**: The Clerisy founded itself during the Extinguishing with the **first Great Tithing** (`recorded as the titan's wrath`). This year, the cell's Act 1 raids starve the dream and stir tremors, forcing `Othemar` to escalate the yearly rite into a **second Great Tithing**.

### 4.1 Inquisitor-Prelate Nemeth, traced

CANON 3.6 (user 2026-10-25; "refinement" marks the planner's detail on the user's gist).

- Who: the Iron Inquisition's commander of the Crucible Watchpost (Act 1 node 06), infused with prime aberrant marrow,
  "a foe cold steel cannot finish" (cast registry; campaign 4.3). Renamed from "High Inquisitor Bastion". Age unset
  (Q-06).
- Want (refinement): his infusion is the Inquisition's crude aberrant marrow, which wears the body down; he wants the
  Clerisy's pure marrow, rendered from the youngest tithe-children, and a place among the ageless. The captured founder
  is his offering: he gives them to Lazar's anatomists to be made into a saint-weapon against the cell.
- Act 1 M5 (canon): boss of ENC-EVT-06 with 2 Purifiers, 2 Footmen and 2 Watch Crossbowmen; his reinforcements cut
  off the retreat; his executioners take the founder on the winch alive. The objective is escape: the cell does not
  defeat him, and he escapes with the captured founder (Q-25 A).
- Act 2 (proposal; refinement): at 2.4 the cell sees him deliver the founder to Lazar and arrives too late; this
  closes a rescue in practice (Q-33 option a).
- Act 3 (proposal): node 20 the Silent Ossuary, one battle in two phases (user 2026-10-25): (1) the founder, already
  corrupted by Lazar's anatomists, fights at Nemeth's side against the cell, with their old moves twisted and lines
  that answer the cell by name (refinement); (2) fighting their old teammates breaks the founder: they go insane and
  kill everyone in reach, Nemeth and his guard included, and the cell fights the monstrosity alone (build: Mantle
  Fissure 4.4). The win offers mercy and the Transcendent Relic; his Warded Cassock drops where he dies. Node 20 is
  optional; his fate if it is skipped is not written (G-15).
- Open: his kit (Q-74). Whether officers otherwise take the Clerisy's marrow is Q-06 / Q-03.
