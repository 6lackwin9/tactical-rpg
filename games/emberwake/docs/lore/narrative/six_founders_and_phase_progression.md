## 1. Summary (`[LOCKED]`)

All six base classes are authored Act 1 founders (`18-24` Unsealed, **100% clean mortal humans on Day 1**) filling the Sluice Undercroft's six starting bunks (`Elyse` joins post-slice in `M4`):

1. **Act 1 — *The Young Vanguard* (`Sunk Ward`):** Cold Steel only (`0` Strain/Calcification). Marrow power and cost are foreshadowed twice (`Quell`'s neck valve in `M2`; `Sabina`'s raw marrow dose on `1` founder in the `Interlude` -> starts Act 2 with a Tier I implant). `M5` Crucible captures `1` of the `4` deployed founders on the winch as `Nemeth` shrugs off Cold Steel.
2. **Act 2 — *The First Rupture* (`High Bastion`):** `Talia` shelters `1` class-matched `18-24` visored/cowled runaway (`6/6` bunks -> `8` via `Berek`). Unlocks `Channel`/`Strain`/`Calcification`, `Sabina`'s `M2` `Slab Retorts` ossuary bone-pins, `Berek`'s weapon durability/masterwork techniques, curfew-bell hazards (`CL-05`), and the **Preservationist (`Berek`) vs. Flesh-Broker (`Sabina`)** Doctrine divide.
3. **Act 3 — *The Awakening Abyss* (`Mantle Fissure`):** Hard Doom Clock (`min 12d`), **Mantle Resonance** (`+15` start Strain + pulse; halved by `3.3` Chorus), Stage IV **Isolation Crypts**, `3.4` Silent Ossuary (`M5` captive turned `2x2` Saint-Weapon), `3.5` `Othemar`, and `3.6` **Dreaming Core** catastrophe-stopping endings (**A `Black-Iron Severance`**, **B `The Living Conduit`**, **C `Stone Martyrdom`**).
