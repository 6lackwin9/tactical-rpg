## 2. The four starting dossiers (`[LOCKED]` combat lesson, `[PLACEHOLDER]` backstory, taint & look)

### 2.1 Kaelen (Vanguard, 21)

- Role: junior guardsman of the Seventh Ward Castellan Watch.
- Look: a battered kettle helm; a dented iron breastplate with the cathedral cross chiselled out; a notched billhook
  poleaxe; the left forearm bound in blood-soaked linen.
- Backstory: born to a poor tailor in the Lower Tenements, he took the Castellan oath at 17 for the daily salt-bread
  and a warded tallow candle for his ailing mother. For four years he stood curfew watch, telling himself that order
  kept the ward safe from the horrors of the Shroud.
- Breaking point: at the dawn sweep for the coming Red Tithing his squad was ordered to drag sixteen adolescent
  apprentices from a cellar into cage wagons. When a 15-year-old girl, the daughter of his mother's neighbour, clung to
  his boots, his squad corporal, Fitch, ordered Kaelen to shatter her knees with his billhook pommel. Kaelen broke
  Fitch's jaw, kicked over the lantern and shouted for the children to run into the dark. His commanding officer,
  Captain Vane, leads the hunt for him (0a, then Act 1 M1).
- Taint (backstory, Act 2 hook): white bone spurs pushing through the skin of his left forearm, left by an involuntary
  Inquisition graft trial (when and where: Open). He hides them: a deserter with a graft would be vivisected as a mutant.
- In 0a: a single unit; Active Guard, armour, and Pommel Strike in a guard's windup to break its poise.

### 2.2 Vesper (Gutter-Stalker, 18)

- Role: curfew runner and thief of the Ash-Crawl Tenements.
- Look: a shredded charcoal cowl, a slate-shingle leather jerkin, twin matte-black bodkins held reversed, chimney soot
  smeared across her cheeks.
- Backstory: her parents died of black-lung when she was nine and were rendered at the Tallow Vats. She grew up in the
  heights between the basalt spires and the tenement roofs, running coded bone-ciphers, warded sulfur matches and
  medicine for the black market past the searchlights, and stealing what the runs did not pay. Tess was her runner
  contact.
- Breaking point: an informant paid by Orlov's Tallow Guild sold out her runner ring for two bags of Rock Salt. She came
  home to find three inquisitorial purgers hanging her fellow runners from the chimney flues with iron wire. Armed with
  her bodkins and ash flasks, she slipped into the shadows, vowing to gut every masked inquisitor in the ward.
- Her taint (backstory): her fingertips dissolve into a drifting black mist like cold smoke; when she focuses, her
  shadow pulls away from the light and stretches toward nearby enemies.
- In 0b: Snap strikes, the ash flask's 3x3 smoke zone, Hamstring Sever to root a fleeing foe, disengaging.

### 2.3 Maren (Heretic Acolyte, 23)

- Role: scribe of the Scriptorium of St. Lyra's, defrocked.
- Look: a torn, ink-stained scribe's habit with the Clerisy sigil ripped away; cracked round spectacles; a bronze censer
  crook staff. At the bridge she takes an embalmer's bone-saw from the cart's rack.
- Backstory: six years copying medical and liturgical texts in the scriptorium. She believed the dogma: that the Red
  Tithing was a holy, sorrowful necessity that keeps the dreaming titan asleep and preserves mankind.
- Breaking point: she copied the forbidden texts, a chronicle of the first Great Tithing, held under cover of the
  Extinguishing when the Clerisy was founded (user 2026-10-25, Q-07 option a; [CANON](../CANON.md) 3.6). What she
  remembers of them is an Act 1 hint; 2.4 and 2.6 confirm it. Proctor Quell denounced her; she was
  defrocked under Arch-Prelate Othemar's seal and sent in an iron mortuary cart toward the dissection crypts. She knows
  Sister Aline only by reputation; it was Aline, not Maren, who found the drugged communion draughts.
- Her taint (backstory): blackened veins creeping up her throat and along her jaw from years of marrow-ink; she feels
  eldritch resonance through her teeth.
- In 0c: field sutures, clearing a Stagger, restoring an ally's poise.

### 2.4 Hollis (Arbalestier, 20)

- Role: crag poacher and scout from the limestone ridges outside Cinderreach.
- Look: a stiff hunting brigandine with a heavy horn recoil pauldron on the right shoulder; an oversized lever-action
  steel arbalest; a quiver of notched bone quarrels.
- Backstory: his family lived by hunting feral ridge-hounds and scavenging pre-cataclysm iron. He and his younger
  sister Wren poached church supply wagons on the mountain passes.
- Breaking point: an Inquisition culling detachment surrounded the homestead at midnight. When the Castellan commander
  dragged 14-year-old Wren into the yard, Hollis put an iron quarrel through his throat from the roof rafters, and Wren
  vanished into the fog. Out of bolts, Hollis was beaten down with lead saps and chained in the mortuary cart.
- Taint (backstory, Act 2 hook): a crude night-stalker eye graft in his left eye, cut in himself with a sharpened silver
  cannula and a cave-beast's optic nerve so he could aim in the Shroud's dark. He regrets it. The pupil glows red when he
  aims (visual only).
- In 0c: line of sight, long-range shots, and Pinning Shot to root heavy foes before they close.

## 3. 0c The Bridge: the four meet

- Setup: a heavy iron-barred mortuary cart is stuck at the crown of a narrow stone bridge over the sewer canal. Maren and
  Hollis are chained inside; three Inquisitors and a crossbowman hold the far side `[data]` (ENC-PRO-0C).
- Flow:
  1. Kaelen and Vesper attack the bridge from the rear.
  2. Vesper drops an ash cloud over the crossbowman; Kaelen strikes the padlock off the cage.
  3. Maren and Hollis break out: Hollis snatches an arbalest from a fallen rack, Maren an embalmer's bone-saw and a
     censer.
  4. For the first time the player commands the full four: a front line, a flanker, ranged pins and field sutures.
- Escape: sirens wail and the watchtower searchlights sweep the stones. Vesper jams a crowbar into the drowned drainage
  grate at the bridge foot and levers the bars aside: "Down! If they catch us on the cobblestones, we're ash!" The four
  drop into the black runoff and vanish beneath the city.

## 4. The hearth: the hub induction scene

A quiet dialogue scene in the Sluice Undercroft before Act 1 M1 `[kit hook]` (dialogue or vignette player) `[data]`
(lines). Location: the Water-Lock Basin and the Tinker's Hearth.

The camera pans across the underground vault. Water drips into the Water-Lock Basin. A dull orange glow comes from
Bram's ventless charcoal forge at the Tinker's Hearth. At the Apothecary's Slab, Sister Aline wipes black ichor from her
scalpels. The four wash up on the flagstones, bruised, soaked and bleeding.

- Kaelen slumps onto a stone bench and strips the soaked linen from his left arm; the white bone spurs catch the forge
  light. Maren kneels beside him and traces them with dread.
- **Maren:** "Bone spurs. Grafted, not grown: these are surgical scars. Who did this to you?"
- **Kaelen:** "Garrison surgeons, under an Inquisition seal. They called it a trial. Nobody asked me. How long?"
- **Maren:** "I have only read about this. If it keeps growing, four months before it locks your elbow."
- Vesper huddles by the coals, wringing soot from her hair, her bodkins across her knees.
- **Vesper:** "We don't have four months, soldier. The Red Tithing is in six weeks. The Watch is sealing every surface
  grate in the ward. If we sit down here in the dark, they'll flush the drains with quicklime. If we go up, we need
  steel, salt and targets."
- Hollis sits on an upturned nail keg checking the winch cable of his arbalest; his left eye glows like an ember.
- **Hollis:** "Steel I can use. Plans are for people who intend to grow old. None of us four will see five-and-twenty.
  So let's crack as many iron masks as we can before the stone takes us."
- Bram "Iron-Lung" Karr brings a flanged hammer down on his anvil and throws sparks into the dark.
- **Bram:** "Enough talk. You survived the street sweep. That makes you either very lucky or very dangerous. There are
  six dry bunks in the Hollows behind me. Bread and salt on the shelf. Sleep while you can, young blood. When the next
  cathedral bell tolls, the war begins."

The "four months" line is a story hook with no mechanic: no founder carries a countdown in Act 1.
