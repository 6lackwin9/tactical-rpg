## 1. Summary (`[LOCKED]`)

- **Interactive Prologue (`0a -> 0b -> 0c -> Hearth`):** Introduces the first `4` clean Day 1 human Founders (`Kaelen`, `Vesper`, `Maren`, `Hollis`) and teaches `timeline` combat one mechanic at a time; `Dunstan` (`M3`) and `Elyse` (`M4`) complete the `6`-Founder Act 1 roster (`0` procedural recruits in Act 1).

All Act 2 recruits come from **6 named Soot-Gothic cohorts wearing visors, cowls, scarves, or respirator-goggles** so they never visually clone the `6` barefaced Founders.
- **`25+` Spent Specialists (`Ferrand` `40`, `Herbalist Clemence` `36`):** Stay **bunk-free Water-Lock Basin guests** granting hub perks; they are never sworn into `18-24` combat bunks (`CL-07`).
- **Living Martyrs (`Acts 2-3`):** Rescued `18-24` Stage III-IV prisoners held in `2` **Isolation Crypt** stasis slots (freeing Dry Hollows bunks)—deployed as short-lived shock troops (`Dreamer's Marrow Geode` legacy) or given merciful `Rock Salt` cremation.
