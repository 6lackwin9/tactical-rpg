## 1. Cosmology, Broken Laws of Nature & Historical Timeline

### 1.1 World Laws & Generational Divide (`[LOCKED]` Mechanics/Structure, `[PLACEHOLDER]` Lexicon)

- **The Low Sleep (`Slumbering Root`)**: Cinderreach sits in a volcanic caldera atop a sleeping, dreaming titan ([cosmology](background/cosmology_and_setting.md)). Its pulse drives seismic tremors, boiling tar-bogs, and upward seepage of **raw mutagenic black marrow** (`G-33`: mutates living flesh; distinct from **rendered marrow**).
- **The Shroud & Bell-Time**: Two centuries ago **the Extinguishing** (`b2`: cause stays a mystery) hid sun, moon, and stars behind a bruised-violet petrified ash canopy. Cathedral bells replace day/night (`travel.rs` counts days only). Only **Warded Tallow** (brimstone-treated fat burning yellow-green) repels mist-aberrations; wood or raw coal draws them.
- **Broken Rot & Ossification**: Dead matter never rots into soil—uncremated bodies harden into black stone (**bone-mortar**) or rise over weeks as skinless **Calcified Husks** unless cremated with rationed **Rock Salt**, brimstone, and **Warded Tallow**. Families trapped in **marrow-debt** sell their dead to Tallow Guild vats and bone-mills.
- **The Unsealed (`under 25`) vs. The Spent (`25+`) (`[LOCKED]` Tone Rule)**:
  - **The Spent (`25+`)**: Dulled into docile compliance—not incompetence (`Q-06`)—by bone-sediment communion draughts and tallow smoke; police their own children for salt vouchers and rarely live past `45` (`Agnes` `44` is ancient; `Othemar` `64` is the visible anomaly). Rare Spent who avoided draughts (`Bram` `33`, `Aline` `26`, `Corvo` `29`) keep sharp minds in stiffening, non-combat bodies.
  - **The Unsealed (`under 25`)**: Elastic blood, clear minds. Playable operatives and recruits are **`18-24` only** (`RECRUIT1`). Children (`under 18`: `Pip` `12`, `Lucan` `14`, `Ida` `16`, `Wren` `14`) are **non-combat story stakes only**—never fighters, graft subjects, or on-screen violence victims.
