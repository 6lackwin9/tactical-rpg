================================================================ SOUND BIBLE (paste into Producer memories)
All music is for "Emberwake: A Scarlet Lullaby", a dark-fantasy tactical RPG in a soot-black industrial-gothic city
(the Sunk Ward): tenement rooftops, tallow vats, bone-mills, iron cages, cathedral bells, rain and searchlights. A
theocracy tithes its youths to keep a dreaming titan asleep; a small cell of young deserters fights back.

- Always instrumental unless asked otherwise. Never lyrics. Wordless voices only when asked.
- Palette: low strings (cello, double bass, viola), a solo violin or viola da gamba, hurdy-gurdy, music box and
  celesta, low male choir and a lone child's or woman's wordless voice, church bells and hand bells, struck metal
  (anvil, chains, iron pipes), frame drums and taiko-like low drums, harp, dulcimer, a small pipe organ.
- Avoid: modern pop, EDM, trap, rock drum kits, electric guitar, synth leads, epic trailer "braams", happy major keys.
- Mood: cold, sooty, sorrowful, defiant; tension held, not released; warmth is rare and earned (the hearth).
- The leitmotif "The Scarlet Lullaby": a simple, slightly sad 6/8 cradle tune that circles in D minor (Dorian
  colour) and never fully resolves. The Clerisy sings it to the tithe-children; it returns in many tracks: sweet on a
  music box, cold in the choir, broken on a hurdy-gurdy in battle, warm on the hearth's strings.
- Game use: tracks loop in the game, so keep each one in a single steady mood and tempo with no fade-out ending,
  no big intro build and no outro; 90 to 150 seconds is enough.
