--- Character info
Maren, a defrocked scribe of the Scriptorium of St. Lyra's turned field medic. Long wavy copper-red hair; round wire-rimmed spectacles, one lens cracked; serious, sharp face; blackened veins creeping up her throat and jaw from years of marrow-ink. Torn, ink-stained dark scribe's habit with the holy sigil ripped away; long brown leather apron; belts and bandoliers of glass vials and herbs; brown gloves and boots. She carries a bronze censer on a crook staff and an embalmer's bone-saw.

How she acts: precise, clinical and fast-thinking; she reads a wound or a lie the same way she read manuscripts. She once believed the Clerisy's dogma completely, and its betrayal left her angry, guilty and driven to expose it. Calm hands under pressure: she stitches allies in the middle of a fight. Dry, blunt, impatient with piety; she pushes her spectacles up when she is thinking. She feels eldritch resonance as an ache in her teeth.

Hollis, a crag poacher and scout from the limestone ridges outside Cinderreach. Lean and tired-looking; short messy dark brown hair, light stubble; a scar across his left eye, where a crude night-stalker eye graft makes the pupil glow red when he aims. Stiff dark hunting brigandine with brass rivets; a heavy horn recoil pauldron on his right shoulder; brown leather harness; dark trousers, worn boots. Guarded and wry, slow to warm up, protective of the young. He carries guilt over his younger sister Wren, lost in the fog the night the Inquisition came, and over the eye graft he cut into himself and regrets. Steady hands, never wastes a bolt.
