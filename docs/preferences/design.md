# Design preferences

Owner: Design thread. Firm rules: `docs/decisions/`. Format: preference (date, source).

- Enemy morale is shown as flavour states (Steady, Shaken, Wavering, Broken), never as numbers (2026-10-18, MORALE1 approval).
- Empowered is a story reward, never a boss-fight mechanic; no party buff at a boss phase change (2026-10-18, PACE1).
- Option branches are paused: Design proposes ideas and Tushar reviews and approves them (2026-10-18 08:29).
- One world first: per-world work waits until the Ashlands are complete (2026-10-18).
