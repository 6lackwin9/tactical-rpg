### FB-187: PR5-8 readings for confirmation

- **Spec**: `docs/sessions/PK2_GRAFTS.md` section 2 vs READY row PR5-8 (@ the `PR5-8` squash commit)
- **Kind**: suggestion
- **Status**: open
- **Finding**: (1) Stage passives are **cumulative** (a member at stage N holds the passives of stages 1..N); the spec says "granted while the member is at that stage", but a battle can add a passive and not remove one, so cumulative is the reading where the battle and the next build agree; the per-stage reading lets data bundle earlier symptoms into a later passive, the cumulative one cannot express per-stage passives. (2) Only members advance on a track; an enemy or guest keeps a `calcify` mark but gets no stage passive and does not turn. (3) `pierce_melee` works only under rule `pierce` (as armour piercing) and on planned attacks and estimates, not riposte or `strike_nearest`; any non-ranged hit counts as melee (adjacent spells too). (4) The breach takes hold at once (the READY row and PK1 2.1), where PK2 section 2 says "from the next round". (5) The skill `unmark` targets allies only (normal SUPPORT targeting), not a turned ally as Quenched Cinder Salt can. (6) The unknown-effect-type message keeps its old list (it is in `checks_golden`), so it does not name `forbid_hands` and `pierce_melee` (the schema help does). (7) A hero calming at the battle end (an `unmark` used, the round not started) settles normally. FB-179 Q4 (graft-only AI Channel rounds) is for PR5-9 (grafts).
- **Recommendation**: keep (1)-(7) as built (each is the built default); for (1) the alternative is a per-track `cumulative` flag; for (5) the alternative lets the skill reach a turned ally like the item.
